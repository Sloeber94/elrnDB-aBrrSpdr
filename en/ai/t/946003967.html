Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods

FUNCTIONAL DESCRIPTION

The Body Computer M1 is supplied directly from the battery via line protected by maxifuse F1 of engine compartment control unit B1 at pin 18 of connector F that interfaces with underfacia control unit B2 .

The Body Computer M1 receives an ignition-operated supply (INT) at pin 9 of connector F of the interface with the junction unit under the dashboard B2 : this signal is used, amongst other things, to "wake up the network".

Pins 8 and 9 of connector I of M1 are earthed.

The nodes connected to Body Computer M1 - from pins 36 and 37 of connector B - via the high speed C-CAN network are:

- engine management control unit M10;

- ABS/VDC control unit M50 ;

- automatic transmission control unit M55 and gear selector control unit M53 (only on version with Automatic Transmission);

- or robotized gearbox control unit M54 (on the Robotized Gearbox version);

- steering angle sensor K58;

- yaw sensor K74.

The following nodes are connected to the Body Computer M1 via the B-CAN low speed network:

- underfacia junction unit B2 ;

- instrument panel E50;

- driver's side door control unit M66 , passenger side door M67 ;

- parking sensor control unit M84 ;

- seat control unit H66 and H67;

- hood control unit M187 (Spider only);

- Air Bag control unit M60 .

- climate control sytem control unit M70;

- controls on the steering wheel (via the clock spring D47);

- built-in radio-telephone-navigator P20;

- Bluetooth control unit M162;

- steering lock control unit M89 ;

- TEG Reader H3.
