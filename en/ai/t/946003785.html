Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods

DESCRIPTION

The LOW trim level of the vehicle is fitted with manually adjusted front seats, with the exception of the backrest angle adjustment which is electrical.

A special switch, located on the actual seat. adjusts the "raising" and "lowering" of the backrest if operated in the two different positions.

The seat is also equipped with a lumbar adjustment knob that makes it possible to improve comfort by altering the backrest support: this adjustment is always mechanical and not controlled by electric motors.

The seat movement can only be activated with the ignition on (INT).

The seat moving circuit is protected by two specific fuses located in the luggage compartment fuse box. A relay on each seat controls the motors.
