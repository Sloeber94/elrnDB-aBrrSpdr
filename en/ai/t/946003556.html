1060FA INJECTOR OPERATION CHECK

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

Proceed as follows on the injector presumed to be faulty.

STEP

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Press the START/STOP button and turn off the engine. Disconnec the wiring harness connector from engine control unit connector B.

Press the START/STOP pushbutton. Check that the voltage across Earth and:

  • Pin 16 of the control unit for injector 1

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

  • Pin 2 of the control unit for injector 2

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

  • Pin 1 of the control unit for injector 3

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

  • Pin 17 of the control unit for injector 4

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

  • Pin 18 of the control unit for injector 5

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

is less than 0.3 V

Repair or if necessary replace the electrical wiring between the engine control unit and the injector

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

2

Press the START/STOP button.

Check that the resistance between Earth and:

  • Pin 16 of the control unit for injector 1

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

  • Pin 2 of the control unit for injector 2

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

  • Pin 1 of the control unit for injector 3

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

  • Pin 17 of the control unit for injector 4

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

  • Pin 18 of the control unit for injector 5

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

is greater than 500 kOhms

Move on to Step 6

Move on to Step 3

3

Disconnect the wiring harness connector from the injector.

Check that the resistance between Earth and:

  • Pin 16 of the control unit for injector 1

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

  • Pin 2 of the control unit for injector 2

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

  • Pin 1 of the control unit for injector 3

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

  • Pin 17 of the control unit for injector 4

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

  • Pin 18 of the control unit for injector 5

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

is greater than 500 kOhms

Repair or if necessary replace the electrical wiring between the engine control unit and the injector

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

4

Check that the resistance between Earth and:

  • Pin 47 of the control unit for injector 1

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

  • Pin 31 of the control unit for injector 2

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

  • Pin 46 of the control unit for injector 3

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

  • Pin 33 of the control unit for injector 4

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

  • Pin 48 of the control unit for injector 5

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

is greater than 500 kOhms

Repair or if necessary replace the electrical wiring between the engine control unit and the injector

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

5

Replace the injector

The test can be considered over

6

Disconnect the wiring harness connector from the injector.

Check that the resistance across:

  • Pin 16 and 47 of the control unit for injector 1

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

  • Pin 2 and 31 of the control unit for injector 2

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

  • Pin 1 and 46 of the control unit for injector 3

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

  • Pin 17 and 33 of the control unit for injector 4

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

  • Pin 18 and 48 of the control unit for injector 5

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

is greater than 500 kOhms

Repair or if necessary replace the electrical wiring of the engine control unit

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

7

Check that the resistance across pin 1 of the injector

See N070 INJECTOR

and:

  • Pin 16 of the control unit for injector 1

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

  • Pin 2 of the control unit for injector 2

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

  • Pin 1 of the control unit for injector 3

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

  • Pin 17 of the control unit for injector 4

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

  • Pin 18 of the control unit for injector 5

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

is less than 5 Ohms

Repair or if necessary replace the electrical wiring between the engine control unit and the injector

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

8

Check that the resistance across pin 2 of the injector

See N070 INJECTOR

and:

  • Pin 47 of the control unit for injector 1

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

  • Pin 31 of the control unit for injector 2

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

  • Pin 46 of the control unit for injector 3

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

  • Pin 33 of the control unit for injector 4

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

  • Pin 48 of the control unit for injector 5

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

is less than 5 Ohms

Repair or if necessary replace the electrical wiring between the engine control unit and the injector

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

9

Check that the resistance across pin 1 and 2 of the injector

See N070 INJECTOR

is between 0.3 and 0.7 Ohms

Replace the injector

10

Carry out a visual check on the condition of the injector and connector pipes to ensure it is OK

Replace the faulty component

11

Replace the engine management control unit
