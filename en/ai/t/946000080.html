Applies to: all engines; all production periods

FUNCTIONAL DESCRIPTION

The switch for reversing lights I20 receives a supply controlled by the ignition (INT) from the fuse line F35 of under-dashboard control unit B2.

When reverse gear is engaged, the switch closes and sends a power supply to the reversing light in right rear light cluster F31 (left rear light cluster F30 on right hand drive versions), directly via control unit B2 from pin 7 of connector D.

The activation signal for the reversing light is also sent to Body Computer M1 - pin 3 of connector F - and from there to steering wheel interface H5, for the automatic engagement of the rearscreen wiper.

See E3021 REAR WINDOW WASH/WIPE
