Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods; only with equipment: Gas discharge headlamps

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

With the ignition ON, check for the presence at Pin 9 of the 14-way connector of the Master

See F010 LEFT HEADLAMP

and Slave headlamp

See F011 RIGHT HEADLAMP

of a voltage of +12 V.

The test can be considered over

Move on to Step 2

2

Check the under-dashboard junction unit to ensure that fuse F37 (in the centre of the second row from the top) has not blown

Replace fuse F37 in the under-dashboard junction unit after identifying the reason why it blew

3

Restore wiring continuity between the Body Computer and headlamp

See E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS
