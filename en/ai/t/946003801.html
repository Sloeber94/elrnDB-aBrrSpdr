FUNCTIONAL DESCRIPTION

Radio P20 is supplied directly (pin 7 and 2 of connector A) from the battery via the line for fuse F23 of engine compartment junction unit B1.

Radio P20 receives an ignition-operated (INT) power supply (pin 4 of connector A) via the line for fuse F49 (F51 as of 04/07) of the junction unit under dashboard B2.

Pin 8 of connector A is earthed.

Pins 1 and 3 of connector A receive the CAN line by which the radio P20 is connected to the Body Computer M1 and the other system nodes.

Radio P20 is connected, via the CAN, to the radio controls on the steering wheel, via clock spring D47.

See E1050 CAN CONNECTION LINES

Amplifier P70 receives a direct supply from the battery - at pin 31 - via the line for fuse F54 of the fuse box casing in the boot B45; pin 2 of amplifier P70 is earthed.

Pin 6 of P70 is connected via a line to radio P20 - pin 6 of connector C - which supplies a positive signal to enable the amplifier to be switched on.

The cables with signals for the speakers leave from connector B of P20; these signals are sent to the BOSE amplifier P70 which controls the actual speakers:

- pins 5 and 6 of P20 to pins 7 and 19 of P70 and to the left front speakers P35 (P36 for right hand drive versions) and P45 - pins 31 and 32 of P70

- pins 3 and 4 of P20 to pins 8 and 20 of P70 and to the right front speakers P36 (P35 for right hand drive versions) and P46 - pins 26 and 27 of P70;

- pins 7 and 8 of P20 to pins 9 and 21 of P70 and to left rear speaker P30 - pins 5 and 17 of P70;

- pins 1 and 2 of P20 to pins 10 and 22 of P70 and to right rear speaker P31 - pins 5 and 17 of P70.

The other signals leaving the BOSE amplifier P70 control the specific speakers:

- centerfill P32 - pins 4 and 16 of P70;

- centerfill P49 - pins 3,11, 12 and 15 of P70.

The aerial P25 is connected to the radio by means of the special coaxial cable.

On the Spider version, when the hood is open, hood control unit M187 provides the Bose system with a hood opening signal - pin 24 of P70 - in order to alter the frequency response of the amplifier according to the volume (open or closed) in the passenger compartment

See descriptions 7030H AUTOMATICALLY OPERATED HOOD

A connection lead runs from connector E of P20 to CD player P75. This cable is divided into two sections: one under the dashboard and one under the passenger compartment lining (coupling D96).
