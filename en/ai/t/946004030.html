Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods

STEP

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

REMEDIAL ACTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Solenoid check

With the Examiner in active diagnosis check that the solenoid valves are working properly

Move on to Step 2

Restore the wiring between the control unit and the hydraulic unit

Restore the 20 A fuse F 63

2

Solenoid valve resistance check

Check that the resistance of each solenoid valve in the unit is 12 Ohm ± 5% at 20°C

Replace the hood control unit

Op. 7030H50 AUTOMATIC HOOD ACTIVATING SYSTEM CONTROL UNIT - R.R. FOLLOWING SYSTEM CHECK

Replace the electro-hydraulic unit

Op. 7030H58 AUTOMATIC HOOD HYDRAULIC ACTIVATING SYSTEM MOTOR/PUMP ASSEMBLY - R.R.
