Refitting

1. Open the armrest.

2. Undo the armrest trim bolts.

3. Remove armrest trim (3a), releasing spring (3b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the armrest trim is not damaged.

- Position the armrest trim and attach the spring.

- Tighten the retaining bolts.

- Close the armrest.
