Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Remove the engine oil filler cap.

2. Remove the sound insulation cover from the fastening pins.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the soundproofing cover back in its housing and secure it using the pins.

- Place the engine oil filler cap in its housing.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Release and remove the sound insulation cover from the retaining pins.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the sound insulation cover in position exerting manual pressure at the retaining pins.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove sound insulation cover (1b) from the retaining pins (1c).

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the sound insulation cover in position exerting manual pressure at the retaining pins and tighten the bolts.
