Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS; all production periods

This procedure should be carried out if hydraulic circuit pipes or components have been removed/refitted.

- Place the gear lever in neutral and keep the brake pedal pressed down throughout the operation.

- Connect an Examiner tester and perform the operations described in the specific procedure for bleeding the electrohydraulic gearbox hydraulic system.
