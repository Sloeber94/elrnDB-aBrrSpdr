Op. 1016C10 SINGLE CYLINDER HEAD - R.R. AND REPLACE GASKET

- Rotate the engine until the pistons for cylinders 1 and 4 are at B.D.C.

Push the connecting rods upwards until the pistons are above the cylinder block/crankcase.

Take care not to damage the cylinder liner/bore during the removal of the pistons.

- Lower the vehicle.

- Release and remove the connecting rods and pistons and upper connecting rod half-bearings for cylinders 1 and 4.

- Carry out the same operations for the removal of the connecting rods and pistons for cylinders 2 and 3.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the connecting rods and pistons and upper connecting rod half-bearings for cylinders 2 and 3 using a suitable fitting tool.

- Raise the vehicle.

- Fit the connecting rod caps complete with the half-bearings for cylinders no. 2 and 3 and secure them tightening the bolts to the recommended torque.
