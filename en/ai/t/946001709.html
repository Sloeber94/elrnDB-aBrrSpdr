During a braking manoeuvre, whistling or squeaking can be heard from the wheel area. Vibrations are also felt in the passenger compartment

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Test results

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Steer to the right or to the left and check whether the noise increases.

Move on to Step 1

The noise changes during steering.

Proceed as instructed in the

See table S015 NOISE WHILST DRIVING

1

CHECK THE PAD TYPE AND SURFACE

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check:

  • the condition of the pad braking surfaces;
  • pad type.

Move on to Step 2

1. Front braking surfaces vitrified or with traces of grease, oil or water.

Pads of non-specified type.

1. Re-clean, check and remove the fault causes. Replace the pads if necessary

Op. 3310A60 FRONT BRAKE PADS - REPLACE

2. Rear braking surfaces vitrified or with traces of grease, oil or water.

Pads of non-specified type.

2. Re-clean, check and remove the fault causes. Replace the pads if necessary

Op. 3310B62 REAR BRAKE PADS - REPLACE

2

CHECK DISCS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the brake discs.

End of diagnosis

1. Front discs worn or damaged.

Pads of non-specified type.

1. Regrind the discs or, if necessary, replace them

Op. 3310A48 FRONT BRAKE DISC (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R

2. Rear discs worn or damaged.

Pads of non-specified type.

2. Regrind the discs or, if necessary, replace them

Op. 3310B48 REAR BRAKE DISC (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.
