7050A78 SKI FLAP IN REAR SEAT BACKREST - R.R.

Removing

Refitting

1. Lower the armrest.

2. Undo the ski holder flap fixing bolts.

3. Release the side retainers.

4. Raise the armrest and lower the left backrest after having released the lock.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Remove the ski holder flap.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. If necessary, remove the frame (1a) for the ski holder flap, working as described below.

2. Lift up the backrest and lower the armrest, then fold the upper part of the frame (1a) gently in position (2a) and extract the actual frame releasing the inner retainers (2b).

To fold the frame (1a) more easily, heat it using an air heater (drier).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the ski holder flap is not damaged.

- If removed, place the ski holder flap frame in position and attach the retainers.

- Lift up the armrest and lower the backrest.

- Place the ski holder flap in position and attach the side retainers.

- Tighten the ski holder flap fixing bolts.
