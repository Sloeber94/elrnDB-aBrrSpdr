Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods

The pipe from the fuel tank to the activated carbon filter consists of a front section and a rear section joined by quick couplings.The front section of the pipe fitted to the fuel tank is built into the multifunction valves which are part of the tank, so refer toOp. 1040A10 FUEL TANK - R + R AND DECANT FUELThe rear section is built into the activated carbon filter, so refer toOp. 1080E20 ACTIVE CARBON FILTER FOR FUEL VAPOURS - R + R
