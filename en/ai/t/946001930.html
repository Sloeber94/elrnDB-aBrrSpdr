Removing

Refitting

1. Undo the nut (1a) fixing the bracket (1b) to the rigid high pressure pipe joining the single fuel manifold pipes (1c).

2. Undo the connectors and remove the rigid high pressure pipe joining the single fuel manifold pipes (1c).

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the rigid high pressure pipe joining the single fuel manifold pipes in position and tighten the connector.

- Tighten the nut fixing the bracket to the rigid high pressure pipe joining the single fuel manifold pipes.
