Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition ON, the engine oil temperature warning light in the additional instrument panel is not working;

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

the engine oil temperature faulty signal is made available to the instrument panel and, via a serial line, to the gauge.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Keeping the Examiner connected to the diagnostic socket, check the operation of the warning light on the instrument panel by means of active diagnosis

Move on to Step 4

1.The warning light does not come on for the serial line with the NQS interrupted

1.Renew serial line

2.Instrument panel defective

2.Restore power supply

3.Instrument power supply interrupted

3.Replace additional instrument panel

Op. 5560C51 COMBINED ANALOGUE CONTROL PANEL - R+R OR REPLACE

4.Instrument defective

4.Replace gauge

Op. 5560C51 COMBINED ANALOGUE CONTROL PANEL - R+R OR REPLACE

2

ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the continuity/insulation of the lead between oil temperature sensor and the instrument panel

See E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL

Move on to Step 5

1.Cable interrupted/not insulated

1. Restore cable continuity/insulation

2.Oil temperature sensor defective

2.Replace oil temperature sensor
