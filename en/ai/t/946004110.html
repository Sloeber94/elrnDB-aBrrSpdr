Refitting

1. Using the electric control, place 5th arc (1a) in the raised position (as illustrated), hood compartment cover (1b) in a fully raised position and side covers (flaps) (1c) in a lowered position (as illustrated).

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods

- Discharge the hydraulic system pressure.

Op. 0010T79 HOOD HYDRAULIC SYSTEM - DISCHARGE AND RESTORE PRESSURE

1. Mark the position of retaining bands (1a) in relation to the pipes and wiring (as a reference for refitting).

2. Cut the retaining bands.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods

1. Working on the hydraulic actuator, undo bolt (1a) and remove plate (1b).

2. Disconnect pipe (2a) from actuator (2b).

Seal the connectors that have been disconnected using suitable plugs to prevent the oil from escaping and foreign bodies from entering the system.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods

2. Remove lining (1b) by releasing retainers (2b).

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods

1. Remove the hydraulic control unit sound insulation in the hood housing compartment.

2. Raise the side trim slightly.

3. Open retaining bands (3a) for pipe (3b) to be removed.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods

1. Working on the hydraulic control unit, undo bolts (1a) and remove plate (1b) in the direction of the arrow, to release the pipes.

2. Disconnect pipe (2a) from hydraulic control unit (2b).

Seal the connectors that have been disconnected using suitable plugs to prevent the oil from escaping and foreign bodies from entering the system.

3. Remove the hood 5th hoop hydraulic actuator extension pipe.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the hood 5th hoop hydraulic actuator extension pipe is not damaged.

Remove the plugs from the connectors and clean the connectors; check that the seals are effective.

- Position the hood 5th hoop hydraulic actuator extension pipe.

- Connect the pipe to the hydraulic control unit.

- Move the plate into the pipe retaining position, then tighten the bolts.

- Fit the retaining bands along the route of the pipe in the hood housing compartment.

- Position the side trim.

- Connect the pipe to the actuator, then fit the plate and secure it using the bolt.

- Fit the wiring and pipe retaining bands, ensuring they are in the correct positions marked when dismantling.

When fitting the bands, position the wiring and the pipes as close as possible to one another to prevent interference with moving parts.

- Restore the hydraulic system pressure.

Op. 7030H03 HOOD MANUAL OPENING FOLLOWING DEACTIVATION OF HYDRAULIC SYSTEM - CARRY OUT PROCEDURE

- Carry out several opening/closing cycles of the hood to bleed the hydraulic system.

Check that there is no interference with the moving parts in the areas where the refitted pipe moves together with the hood.

Op. 0010T81 HOOD HYDRAULIC SYSTEM FLUID - CHECK LEVEL AND TOP UP IF NECESSARY

- Check that the hood is working correctly.
