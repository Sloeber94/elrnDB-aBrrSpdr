1. Disconnect the rapid connector for the brake servo vacuum intake pipe, vacuum pump side.

2. Disconnect the vacuum reservoir vacuum pump pipe, vacuum pump side.

3. Undo the bolts (3a) and remove the vacuum pump (3b) complete with gasket.
