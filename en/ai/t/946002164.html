Removing

- Refit the handbrake cables by passing through the bulkhead coupling passage and positioning the seal.

- Connect the handbrake control cable to the rear brake calipers.

- Refit the brackets securing the handbrake cables to the underbody and secure with bolts.

- Refit the brackets securing the handbrake cables to the body and secure with nuts.

- Connect the cables to the handbrake lever.
