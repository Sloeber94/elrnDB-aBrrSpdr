The fuel consumption figures given in the table have been determined in accordance with the fuel consumption measurement requirements in directive 1999/100/CE.

These figures have been defined during the course of the homologation tests.

The test conditions include:

- an urban cycle which includes a cold start followed by a varied, simulated urban cycle;

- an extra-urban cycle which includes frequent acceleration, in all gears, simulating normal out-of-town vehicle usage. The speed is between 0 and 120 km/h;

- the average combined fuel consumption is made up of 37% urban cycle and 63% extra-urban cycle.
