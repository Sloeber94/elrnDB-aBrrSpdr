Refitting

Removing

The gear engagement position sensor should be preloaded during the fitting stage and therefore proceed as described below.

1. Rotate the engagement pin in a clockwise direction until it is in the end of travel position.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS; all production periods

1. Position the sensor complete with O-ring as illustrated in order to engage the pin positioned previously in line with the reference mark on the sensor.

2. Rotate in a clockwise direction (preloading) until the openings are aligned and then press the sensor into place.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS; all production periods

1. Tighten the bolts (1a) fixing the gear engagement position sensor (1b) to the recommended torque.
