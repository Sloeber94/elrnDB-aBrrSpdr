7030HE CHECK ON OPERATION OF FLAP CONTROL MOTOR

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods; only with equipment: Spider

STEP

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

REMEDIAL ACTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Check using Examiner

Using the Examiner in the "Selection" ambient, select "Def flap actuator" and check that the flap is working properly (open, load, closed)

Move on to Step 2

Replace the flap kinematic components if damaged

Op. 7210E47 HOOD HOUSING COMPARTMENT SIDE COVER - R.R.

2

Check the wiring

Check the continuity of the wiring between the flap motor and the hood control unit

Restore the correct wiring between the hood control unit and the flap motor

3

Check on motor winding resistance

Disconnect the motor connector

Using the Examiner connect to pins 5 and 6 and check that the motor winding resistance is around 35 Ohm ±5%

Replace the flap motor

Op. 7210E47 HOOD HOUSING COMPARTMENT SIDE COVER - R.R.

4

Potentiometer power supply check

Using the Examiner in the voltmeter mode, connect the probes to pins 1 and 3 of the flap motor and check that the voltage is around 5 Volts

Replace the hood control unit

Op. 7030H50 AUTOMATIC HOOD ACTIVATING SYSTEM CONTROL UNIT - R.R. FOLLOWING SYSTEM CHECK

5

Potentiometer signal check

With the Examiner in the voltmeter mode, connect the probes to pins 1 and 2 and check that with the flap completely open the voltage is around 4.3 V whilst with the flap closed it should be around 1.2 Volts

Replace the flap motor

Op. 7210E47 HOOD HOUSING COMPARTMENT SIDE COVER - R.R.

Connector pin out

1 - Potentiometer negative

2 Potentiometer signal

3 + 5 V potentiometer power supply

4 Free

5 + 12 V Engine power supply

6 - Engine negative
