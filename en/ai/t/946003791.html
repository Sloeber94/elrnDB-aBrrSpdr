FUNCTIONAL DESCRIPTION

The hood control unit M187 receives a power supply - at pins 2 and 4 of connector B - via two lines protected by fuses F62 and F63 located in the fuse box in the boot B45.

Pins 1 and 3 of connector B of M187 are earthed.

The hood control unit M187 is connected via the CAN line to the Body Computer M1 and to the other network nodes - pins 1 and 9 of the connector

It is connected, in particular, to the instrument panel E50, signalling alarm conditions and errors or faults detected by means of the dedicated warning light

The hood control unit M187 receives signals from different sensors thereby keeping the movements of the hood and the hood cover under control.

The hood opening/closing control H130 sends two earth signals to pins 11 and 3 of connector A of M187 to indicate the hood opening and closing request respectively.

The Hall effect sensors in the hood closing lock K197 right side and K198 left side, send two signals to indicate the hood "resting" (end of movement travel), pins 12-14 of connector a of M187 and the correct closure of the hood, pins 4-6 of connector A of M187. Pin 13 of connector A provides a power supply for the Hall effect sensors.

The hood arch position Hall effect sensor K199, receiving a power supply from the control unit from pin 15 of connector D of M187, sends three signals to indicat that the arch is raised, in the intermediate position or lowered to pins 7, 1 and 3 respectively of connector D of M187.

The hood folded Hall effect sensor K200, receiving a power supply from the control unit from pin 15 of connector D of M187, sends a signal to pin 9 of connector D of M187.

The hood cover position Hall effect sensor K203, receiving a power supply from the control unit from pin 16 of connector D of M187, sends two signals to indicate the hood cover reasing - to pin 11 of connector C of M187 - and the hood cover raised - to pin 8 of connector D of M18.

The Hall effect sensors in the two hood cover locks K201 left and K202 right, receive a power supply form the control unit from pin 14 of connector D of M187 and send two signals to pins 4 and 2 respectively of connector D of M187.

The control unit M187 controls the opening of the hood flaps via two motors N190 left and N191 right, controlled by pins 2-13 and 1-12 of connector C of M187; a potentiometer "returns" the position of the flaps to the control unit, to pins 17 and 6 respectively of connector C of M187, whilst a power supply and reference earth are sent from pins 5-18 and 7-16.

Lastly, the control unit M187 controls the electrohydraulic unit N192 which moves the hood via three solenoid valves, whilst a pump provides the necessary pressure for the hydraulic circuit: the pump is controlled by pins 1 and 2 of connector E of M187, the valves by pins 2-8, 3-9 and 4-10 of connector F of M187.

An NTC sensor is connected to pins 1-7 of connector F of M187 which controls the temperature of the hydraulic pump.

The control unit M187 is also connected - from pin 16 of connector A - to the BOSE amplifier P70, pin 24, which modifies the frequency response to compensate for losses that occur when the hood is open.
