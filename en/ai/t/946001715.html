With the vehicle moving, noise can be heard coming from one of the front doors

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Test results

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

First of all check that the moving window is correctly shut.

Check that the wind noise is actually coming from one of the front doors.

Move on to Step 1

Continue following the instructions in the table

Continue following the instructions in the table

See table T006 WINDSCREEN WIND NOISE

The wind noise is coming from the lower part of the passenger compartment.

Continue following the instructions in the table

See table T010 PASSENGER COMPARTMENT WIND NOISE, LOWER AREA (UNDERBODY)

The wind noise is coming from the rear area.

Continue following the instructions in the table

Operation

Check that the weatherstrip/dust seal is correctly fitted.

locate the area where the wind noise is coming from.

The wind noise persists with the entire door taped

Check for sealant between the wing and the pillar under the wheel arch liner

The wind noise is coming from the area between the pillar and the windscreen

Check the condition of the door housing seal in that area and that it adheres correctly to the bodyshell, if necessary, replace it

Adjust the door hinges

Op. 7005A14 FRONT SIDE DOOR - ADJUST CENTERING AND MOULDING - INCLUDES ADAPTATION OF HINGES AND SEALS ON DOOR COMPARTMENT

The wind noise is coming from the area of the outer glass scraper

Check the condition of the glass scaper and make sure it is correctly fitted
