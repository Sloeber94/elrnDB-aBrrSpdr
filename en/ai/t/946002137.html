Removing

Refitting

1. Loosen the band and disconnect the vacuum pipe from the intake chamber.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS; all production periods

1. Disconnect the single-acting valve from the brake servo.

- Remove the vacuum pipe complete with single-acting valve.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Position the vacuum pipe complete with single-acting valve.

- Fit the single-acting valve in the brake servo housing.

- Connect the vacuum pipe to the intake chamber and secure it using the band.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Op. 1016A10 SOUND-PROOFING COVER - R.R

1. Disconnect the rapid connector for the vacuum intake pipe for the brake servo, vacuum pump side.

2. Release the brake servo vacuum pipe from the fastenings.

3. Withdraw the brake servo single-acting valve from the brake servo.

4. Remove the vacuum pipe assembly for the brake servo.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Refit the brake servo vacuum pipe assembly in its seat.

- Refit the brake servo single-acting valve in its seat on the brake servo.

- Secure the brake servo vacuum pipe using the collars.

- Connect the quick-release fitting for the brake servo vacuum intake pipe, vacuum pump side.

Op. 1016A10 SOUND-PROOFING COVER - R.R

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods

The vacuum pipe is incorporated in the brake servoc one-way valve circuit and therefore it is necessary to refer toOp. 3330D28 VACUUM PIPE COMPLETE WITH SINGLE-ACTING VALVES - R.R.
