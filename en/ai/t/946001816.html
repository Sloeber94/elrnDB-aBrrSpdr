Refitting

Removing

- Clean any residues of gasket from the the cylinder head - engine block matching surfaces.

- Carry out a visual inspection of the components.

- The lower cylinder head plane can be refaced if the flatness is betweeb 0.05 and 0.15 mm.

- The refacing does not have to be carried out for lower flatness values.

- The refacing is not permitted for higher flatness values.

- Before refacing it is necessary to check that the minimum depth for each combustion chamber is 12.75 mm (at the point shown in the diagram) in order to guarantee the minimum combustion chamber volume.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS; all production periods

- Place the cylinder head gasket in position.

The gasket should be positioned with the word "TOP" facing upwards.

- Place the cylinder head in position.

Two mechanics are needed for this operation.- Check that the centering bushes are correctly positioned.Take care to raise the timing chain.

1. Tighten the cylinder head fixing bolts to the recommended torque using the angular tightening tool.

Follow the order shown in the figure for each tightening sequence.

Ensure the timing chain is not stuck.

3. Fit the timing chain on the inlet side camshaft phase transformer.

The light blue mesh (3a) should be in line with the reference (3b) on the inlet side camshaft phase transformer.

4. Place the exhaust side camshaft phase transformer (4a) in its housing and secure it using a new bolt (4b) without tightening it.

Rotate the exhaust side camshaft using the hexagon until the pink mesh (4c) is in line with the reference (4d) on the exhaust side camshaft phase transformer.

Check that the timing chain is correctly fitted on the drive gear (on the crankshaft) checking that there are three chain links between the head support surface and the timing references on the phase transformers.
