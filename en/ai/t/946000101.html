Applies to: all engines; all production periods

FUNCTIONAL DESCRIPTION

On the basis of the battery voltage (SBMT) described previously, the Body Computer M1 supplies the boot light G40 from pin 23 of connector E and the driver's sun visor light G31 and the passenger sun visor light G32 from pin 33 of connector E; the glove compartment light G30 is supplied in a similar manner from pin 16 connector H.

The boot opening switch is located in the lock N47 and sends an earth signal with the boot open to light up the relevant courtesy light G40; the same signal is sent to the Body Computer M1 - pin 36 of connector E "boot open" signal; the same signal is used by the alarm system, when fitted.

See E7015 ALARM

the glove compartment light G30 is switched on automatcially when the switch I30 closes when the compartment is opened.

The lights in the visors G31 and G32 are switched on by an earth signal from the switch on the actual lights.
