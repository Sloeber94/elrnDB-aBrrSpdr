Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS; all production periods

- Carry out a visual inspection of the pipes, electric pump, reservoir, hydraulic unit, actuators (engagement/selection, clutch) whilst carrying out 50 gear changes from 1st to 2nd/Reverse and from 2nd/Reverse to 1st (with a frequency of 1 gear change every 3-4 secs).

- After restoring the system seal, bleed the clutch using Examiner (in active diagnosis mode).
