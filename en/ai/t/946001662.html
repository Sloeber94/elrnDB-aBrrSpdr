Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The seat cushion and front backrest heater pad is not working / not working properly.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Test results

1

SEAT CONNECTION CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the connection between the seat connection and the floor connection

Move on to Step 2

Heater pad supply connector detached from the connection on the vehicle floor

Fit the seat heater pad connector correctly with the vehicle floor connector

2

CHECK ON CONNECTIONS FOR CUSHION HEATER PAD AND BACKREST HEATER PAD

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the cushion heater pad connector and backrest heater pad connector are correctly attached

Move on to Step 3

Cushion heater pad connector detached from backrest heater pad connector

Fit the cushion and backrest connectors correctly

3

HEATER PAD BUTTON CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the connector is correctly inserted in the heater pad button, also check for any oxidation/impurities at the connector/button

Move on to Step 4

1.Connector not correctly inserted in the heater pad button

2. Presence of oxidation/impurities at button connector

1. Refit connector properly in heater pad button

2. Eliminate oxidation/impurities at heater pad button connector

4

HEATER PAD WIRING CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check for any breaks/tears in the cushion heater pad and backrest heater pad wiring

Move on to Step 5

1.Presence of cuts/tears in the heater pad supply leads

2. Presence of cuts/tears in the backrest heater pad supply leads

1. Replacement of cushion heater pad

Op. 7045M22 HEATER PAD FOR FRONT SEAT CUSHION, LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R. FOLLOWING ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

2. Replacement of backrest heater pad

Op. 7045M26 FRONT SEAT BACKREST HEATER PAD, LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R. FOLLOWING ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

5

CHECK ON OPERATION OF CUSHION AND BACKREST HEATER PAD RESISTANCE

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the resistance of the cushion heater pad and backrest heater pad is working properly

End of diagnosis

1. Cushion heater pad resistance broken

2. Backrest heater pad resistance broken

1. Replacement of cushion heater pad

Op. 7045M22 HEATER PAD FOR FRONT SEAT CUSHION, LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R. FOLLOWING ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

2. Replacement of backrest heater pad

Op. 7045M26 FRONT SEAT BACKREST HEATER PAD, LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R. FOLLOWING ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK
