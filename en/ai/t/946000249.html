Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Connect Nav+

FUNCTIONAL DESCRIPTION

Radio P20 is supplied directly (pin 7 of connector A) from the battery via the line for fuse F23 of engine compartment control unit B1.

Radio P20 receives an ignition-operated (INT) power supply (pin 4 of connector A) via the line for fuse F49 (F51 as of 04/07) of under-dashboard control unit B2.

Pin 8 of connector A is earthed.

Pins 1 and 3 of connector A receive the CAN line by which radio P20 is connected to Body Computer M1 and the other system nodes.

The control buttons located on the front courtesy light G10 - connector G - are connected to pins 10 and 12 of connector D of P20.
