Removing

Refitting

- Place the front seat in the front end of travel position and move the backrest forward.

1. Adjust lock (1a) and open compartment cover (1b).

2. Undo the bolts.

3. Remove the compartment.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods; only with equipment: Spider

- If necessary, remove the cylinder lock as follows.

1. Undo the bolts securing the lock mount.

2. Remove the lock assembly.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods; only with equipment: Spider

1. To retract lock retainers (1a), release them from mount housings (1b).

2. Keep the lock.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods; only with equipment: Spider

1. Using two drifts (1a), retract ends (1b) of retaining spring (1c) and extract cylinder lock (1d).

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods; only with equipment: Spider

Refitting

Removing

- If removed, refit the cylinder lock as follows.

- Check that the cylinder is not damaged.

- Place the cylinder in position and attach to the retaining clip.

- Attach the lock retainers of the lock mount housing.

- Position the lock assembly and tighten the bolts.

- Check that the lock is operating correctly.

- Check that the storage compartment is not damaged.

- Position the storage compartment.

- Tighten the bolts.

- Close the compartment cover.

- Move the front seat to an intermediate position.
