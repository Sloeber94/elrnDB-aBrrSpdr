0010T64 BRAKE AND/OR CLUTCH FLUID CHECK LEVEL AND TOP UP AS NECESSARY

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- Check that the brake/clutch fluid in the reservoir is at the maximum level; if this is not the case, top up using the recommended fluid.

- When topping up the brake/clutch fluid, remove the reservoir cap and top up until the maximum level is reached.

Qty.

Hydraulic brake and clutch fluid circuit

TUTELA TOP 4

FMVSS n. 116 - DOT 4 - ISO 4925 - SAE J-1704 - CUNA NC 956-01

0.50

The fluid in the reservoir should not exceed the MAX notch.

The symbol on the tank identifies synthetic type brake fluids, distinguishing them from mineral type fluids. Using mineral type fluids irreparably damages the special braking system rubber seals.

Take great care to prevent the brake/clutch fluid, which is extremely corrosive, from coming into contact with the paintwork. If this does happen, was immediately with water.

Brake fluid is poisonous and highly corrosive. In the event of accidental contact, immediately wash the affected parts with water and neutral soap. Then rinse thoroughly. Consult a doctor immediate if you swallow the fluid.
