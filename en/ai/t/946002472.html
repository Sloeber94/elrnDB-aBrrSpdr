5580E17 STEERING LOCK - R.R

Path
Procedures > 55 ELECTRICAL EQUIPMENT > 5580 SPECIAL ACCESSORIES > 5580E ANTI-THEFT DEVICE > 5580E17 STEERING LOCK - R.R
Kind
repair procedure (removal / refitting; the operation number is the code)
Applies to
all engines; all production periods
Interactive page

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- With the electronic key (TEG) inserted, disconect the battery. In this way, the steering lock remains disengaged.

1. Undo the bolts.

2. Remove the lower steering column trim.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Disconnect the electrical connection.

Description

Component

STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

2. Remove the shear bolts using a drift and mallet.

3. Remove steering lock device (3a) releasing it from brackets (3b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the steering lock device is not damaged.

- Position the steering lock device in its seat.

- Screw in and tighten the new special bolts until the heads shear off.

- Connect the electrical connection.

- Position the lower steering column cover and tighten the fixing bolts.

Several of the CAN nodes, including the instrument panel, are programmed with default settings that customers will find when they acquire the vehicle. If the panel is replaced, the Parts Dept. will send a blank component. once fitted, the data memorized in the Body Computer must be transferred to it by carrying out the PROXY ALIGNMENT procedure using the Examiner.

- Check that the steering lock is working.
