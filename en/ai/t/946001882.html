Spanner

Loosen/ tighten ring nut securing submerged pump assembly complete with level gauge control

Take care because the ring nut will lift as soon as it is released from the tank.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods; only with equipment: Brera

1. Lift up the submerged fuel assembly complete with level gauge.

2. Detach the additional fuel level sensor electrical connection.

3. Disconnect the quick coupling of the connecting pipe between the pump supply and additional unit.

4. Open the clips and disconnect the fuel draught pipe from the additional compartment.

- Remove the submerged pump assembly.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods; only with equipment: Brera

- Remove the O-ring.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods; only with equipment: Brera

Refitting

Removing

- Place a new O-Ring seal in the housing on the fuel tank collar and make sure that it is correctly positioned.

- Connect the quick coupling of the connecting pipe between the pump supply and additional unit.

- Connect the fuel draught pipe from the additional compartment to the clips.

- Secure the additional fuel level sensor electrical connection.

- Place the submerged pump assembly in position, complete with level gauge, respecting the references and arrows stamped on it and on the tank.

- With the tool used for the removal, tighten the ring nut securing the pump assembly.
