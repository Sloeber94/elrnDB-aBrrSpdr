FUNCTIONAL DESCRIPTION

Connection D75 receives the direct supply - pin 9 - from the battery via the line for fuse F39 of under-dashboard control unit B2 (this power supply "passes through" Body Computer M1, pin 26 of connector H).

It receives an ignition-controlled power supply (INT) - pin 7 - via the line for fuse F49 (F51 as of 04/07) of control unit B2.

The power supply with the side lights on reaches pin 8 coming from the Body Computer M1, pin 18 of connector H: the telephone, if fitted, is lit up like the other controls

See E2535 CONTROL LIGHTS

Pin 10 of connection D75 is earthed.

Pins 4 and 3 of D75 provide the audio signal (+ and - respectively) for the radio P20 - pins 7 and 8 of connector D, which then controls the speakers.

See E3510 RADIO

Pin 1 of D75 is also connected to the radio P20, pin 9 of connector D (MUTE function).

The hands free microphone located in the front courtesy light G10 - connector D - is connected to pins 5 and 6 of D75.
