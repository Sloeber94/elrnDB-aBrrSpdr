Excessive force, sticking when the release lever is operated

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Test results

1

RELEASE LEVER CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check for interference in the correct sliding of the release lever in its trim

Move on to Step 2

Interference of the release lever with the cover/upholstery during operation, the lever does not return to the rest position

Remove the plastic parts eliminating the interference (excess fabric/upholstery)

2

RELEASE UNIT ALIGNMENT CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the alignment between the release unit and the slot in the plastic trim is correct

Move on to Step 3

Lever distorted, interference with plastic trim

Reposition the lever aligning it with the slot in the trim

3

CHECK ON THE BACKREST

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

After partly removing the upholstery in the lower part of the backrest, check the rivetting of the pin on the control lever and its condition (left and right sides)

Move on to Step 4

Excessive rivetting, pin locked, control lever broken

Replace the backrest assembly

Op. 7045A18 FRONT SEAT BACKREST ASSEMBLY (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

4

CHECKS ON BACKREST ADJUSTER THIRD PLATE

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check for weld residues, dirt on the backrest adjuster third plate

Move on to Step 5

Presence of foreign bodies, weld residues

Clean removing the weld residues or other causes of interference

5

CHECK ON CORRECT LAYOUT OF BOWDEN CONTROL CABLE

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the following, in order:

- the correct layout of the bowden control cable;

- the correct attachment of the outer casing retaining clip on the backrest;

- the condition of the release device in the upper area

End of diagnosis

1. Layout not correct

2. Outer casing retaining clip not correctly attached/released at the backrest

3. Release device in upper area broken/damaged (release lever)

1. Renew the correct routing of the control cables/outer casings

2. Refit the clip in the backrest frame

3. Replace the assembly

Op. 7045A88
