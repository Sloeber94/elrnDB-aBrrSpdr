Removing

Refitting

Op. 7050B12 REAR SEAT HEAD RESTRAINT - R.R.

1. Undo the bolts.

2. Remove the supports (2a) and keep the washers (2b).

3. Release the cover terminal retainers, connected to one another.

4. Remove the hooks.

5. Remove the cover from the upholstery.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the armrest trim is not damaged.

- Fit the cover onto the upholstery.

Mould the cover on the padding during the various refitting stages.

- Fit the hooks.

- Connect the cover terminal retainers to one another.

- Position the supports and the washers, then tighten the bolts.

Op. 7050B12 REAR SEAT HEAD RESTRAINT - R.R.
