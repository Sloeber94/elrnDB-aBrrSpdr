5530B40 SUPPLY BOX ON BATTERY (LINK BATTERY AND FUSE BOX) - R R

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Open the bonnet.

1. Release the retainers (1a) and remove the cover (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Disconnect the electrical connections (1a, 1b, 1c, 1e, 1f).

Description

Component

MAXI FUSE BOX ON BATTERY

See B099 MAXI FUSE BOX ON BATTERY

2. Undo the bolts.

3. Remove the retaining bracket.

4. Loosen the nut for the positive battery terminal.

5. Remove the supply box.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the supply box is not damaged.

1. Place the supply box in position and attach the retainers.

2. Fasten the positive battery terminal using the nut.

Component

Fastening

dia

Value (daNm)

Validity

BATTERY POSITIVE TERMINAL

Nut

M6

0.35 ÷ 0.55

3. Place the retaining bracket (3a) in position and tighten the bolts (3b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- Connect the electrical connections.

- Close the cover and attach the retainers.

- Close the bonnet.

- Test the operation of the electrical system
