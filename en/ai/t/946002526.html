2. Disconnect the electrical connection for the switch.

3. Keep the mounting and switch.

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the mounting and switch are not damaged.

- Connect the electrical connection for the switch.

- Place the mounting and switch in position and attach the retainers.

- Check that the switch is working properly.
