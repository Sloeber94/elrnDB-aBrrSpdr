Op. 7055B54 UNDER ENGINE PROTECTION/GUARD - R.R.

1. Undo the nut (1a) and disconnect the connector (1b) from the condenser.

2. Remove the pipe.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the pipe with the rapid connector (from the evaporator ) to the compressor and to the condenser is not damaged and that there are no cracks that could compromise the seal.

Replace the O-rings on the connectors. Only use green coloured seals that are resistant to R134a coolant. Lubricate the connector threads using anti-freeze.

1. Place the pipe in position.

2. Position the pipe fitting (2a) on condenser (2b) and tighten nut (2c) to the specified torque.
