Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods

DESCRIPTION

The Spider version is equipped with an electrohyraulic system for the automated movement of the hood which ensures that the driver can open and close the hood quickly and safely without leaving their seat.

The activation of the hood takes place by pressing the dedicated button located on the centre tunnel.

The hood opening/closing cycle takes around 25 seconds.

The manoeuvre is reproduced in the control panel display which illustrates the movement of the hood diagramatically.

The completion of the manoeuvre is siganlled by a buzzer and a message appearing in the display.

See descriptions 5560 INSTRUMENTS

An electronic control unit controls the entire electrohydraulic system which carries out the hood automatic opening/closing cycle.

Numerous sensors are located in the hood and hood cover locks and in the hood activating cylinders.

Thanks to the information received from the sensors, the control unit controls the hydraulic system for the cylinders for the hood, the arch, the front locks and the hood cover unlocking/locking the locks for the hood and the hood cover.

The "Boot Hood Node" control unit is located in the passenger compartment on the left side under the mat in the hood compartment and it converses through the vehicle CAN with the other network nodes.

The "Boot Hood Node (NVB-C)" supervises the following functions:

  • input from the hodd activating button;
  • hydraulic movement control (Hall effect sensors);
  • flap movement control;
  • alarm conditions check (e.g. vehicle speed, system internal errors, etc.) providing a warning via the special warning light and messages in the instrument panel
  • interaction with other control units for the optimum management of the system: door nodes (middle drop), climate control node (heated rear windscreen), Body Computer, BOSE system (hood open or closed);
  • auto-diagnostic functions.

The control unit also detects possible errors, that may be temporary (which involve a break in the operating cycle, which can then be restored) or permanent (which require a repair operation to the hood system).

There is a manual emergency procedure which allows the hood to be closed.

Only move the hood with the vehicle stationary because the hood should not be opened/closed whilst the vehicle is moving: if there has been an accidental attempt to activate the hood whilst the vehicle is moving, this manoeuvre will be automatically prevented.

For more details:

See descriptions 7030H AUTOMATICALLY OPERATED HOOD

The hood activating circuit is protected by two special fuses in the boot control unit.
