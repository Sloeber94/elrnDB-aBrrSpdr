1. Undo the fastenings (1a) and remove the engine oil heat exchanger mount (1b).

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Refit the engine oil heat exchanger mount in its seat and tighten the fastenings.

- Place the engine oil heat exchanger complete with pipes in its housing and secure it using the bolts.

- Place the electrical wiring mounting bracket in its housing and secure it using the bolt and the nuts.

- Secure the electrical wiring with the band.

- Connect the electrical connection to the fuel pressure regulator on the air intake/fuel manifold duct.

- Connect the electrical connection of the exhaust side timing sensor.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods
