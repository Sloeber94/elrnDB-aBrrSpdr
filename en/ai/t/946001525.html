Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The side brake lights and the third brake light are activated by the Body Computer Node controlled by the brake light switch.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Connect an Examiner tester and ensure that the following errors are not present in the Body Computer node

Move on to Step 2

Errors present in the Body Computer Node

Proceed as instructed by the diagnostic tester

2

BRAKE LIGHT OPERATION CHECK.

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Keep the diagnostic tester connected and check that the brake lights are operating correctly in active diagnosis mode

Move on to Step 3

Bulbs not operating

Replace the bulb or the associated wiring

3

ELECTRICAL SYSTEM CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the continuity of the bulb wiring

See E2021 BRAKE LIGHTS

Move on to Step 4

Wiring broken

Restore correct wiring

4

CONTROL SWITCH CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the brake switch is working properly by conducting

See Test 5550DA BRAKE PEDAL SWITCH OPERATION CHECK

Move on to Step 5

Brake pedal switch faulty

Replace the brake pedal switch [PR_5550D10]

Op. 5550D10 BRAKE LIGHT SWITCH - R+R

5

BODY COMPUTER CONNECTION CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check for deformed terminals or connections not present on the Body Computer

Move on to Step 6

Missing connections/pins bent

Renew the correct connections

6

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

The fault persists

Replace Body Computer

Op. 5505A32 BODY COMPUTER - R.R
