Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

- It is not possible on this vehicle to remove-refit the single camshaft housing on the vehicle.

The power unit must be removed-refitted in order to carry out this operation.

Op. 1004B10 POWER UNIT WITH MANUAL GEARBOX - R.R

and then the operation of overhauling the engine must be continued at the customized bench.
