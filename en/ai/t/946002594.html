7035B04 REAR WINDSCREEN - ELIMINATE LEAKS

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- Wet the outer part of the rearscreen glass using soapy water.

1. Blow through with compressed air from the inside and identify any penetration points checking along the entire perimeter of the glass.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- If the penetration point is limited in size, seal the area involved from the inside after having removed the components hindering access to the area to be treated.

- Wait for it to dry and repeat the test.

- If penetration point is more extensive, detach and refit the rearscreen.
