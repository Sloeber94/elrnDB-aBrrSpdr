Removing

Refitting

1. Using clippers, cut all the retainers (1a) securing the perimeter of the cushion cover (1b) to the metal frame (1c).

2. Disconnect the ends (2a) of the traction cord (2b), then loosen the cushion cover perimeter (1b).

3. Prise up the cover releasing it from the upholstery perimeter.

4. If necessary, remove the cushion frame (1c) from the upholstery and cover.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Fold the cushion over and, using clippers, cut all the upper cushion cover retainers.

2. Separate the cushion cover (2a) from the upholstery (2b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the cushion cover is not damaged.

- Position the cushion cover on the upper part of the upholstery and attach the retainers using special pliers.

Mould the cover on the padding during the various refitting stages.

- If removed, position the metal frame on the upholstery.

- Fit the cover on the upholstery, along the entire perimter of the cushion.

- Pull the traction cord tensioning the cover, then connect the ends.

- Using special pliers, attach the retainers fastening the perimeter of the cushion cover to the metal frame.
