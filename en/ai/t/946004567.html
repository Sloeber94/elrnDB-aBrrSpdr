Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS; all production periods

STEP

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

REMEDIAL ACTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Solenoid control relay check

Check that the solenoid control relay is working properly

Check the continuity of the 15 A fuse F24 located in the engine compartment junction unit

See E7051 ROBOTIZED GEARBOX

Move on to Step 2

Replace the relay

Replace the fuse after having identified what caused it to blow

2

Check the operation of the solenoid wiring

Disconnect solenoid control relay T20

Insert the TEG in the TEG Reader

Using a cable with a fuse, bridge pin 87 of the connector for relay T20 with pin 30 (as illustrated) and check the operation of the solenoid

See E7051 ROBOTIZED GEARBOX

Move on to Step 3

Restore the wiring between the engine compartment junction unit and the solenoid on the gear lever

Restore the solenoid earth connection.

Replace the gear lever

Op. 2127A12 HAND LEVER FOR MANUAL GEARBOX WITH HYDRAULIC SELECTION - R.R.

3

Check the wiring

Check the continuity of the wiring between the gearbox control unit and the engine compartment junction unit

Check that the gearbox control unit is receiving the correct power supply

Check that the gearbox control unit is correctly earthed

See E7051 ROBOTIZED GEARBOX

Check that the gearbox control unit is working properly

Restore the wiring between the gearbox control unit and the engine compartment junction unit

Restore the correct power supply for the junction unit

Restore the control unit earth connection

Replace the gearbox control unit

Op. 2127E10 HYDRAULIC SPEED SELECTION SYSTEM CONTROL UNIT - R.R.
