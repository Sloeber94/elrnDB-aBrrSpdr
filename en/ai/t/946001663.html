Hardening/creaking of backrest when folding over

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Test results

1

CHECK ON LOWER FASTENINGS MOVEMENT

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the lower backrest fastenings on the vehicle move freely (rapid attachment to wheel arch)

Move on to Step 2

Interference with foreign objects or with fastening elements

Remove the causes of the interference

2

CHECK ON MATCHING BETWEEN BACKREST AND VEHICLE INTERIOR TRIM

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the matching of the backrest with the vehicle interior trim (wheel arch liners, door panels, etc.) folding the backrest over

Move on to Step 3

Interference with trims

Reposition the trims and/or carry out the correct backrest fitting cycle

3

CENTRE BRACKET CONDITION CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the condition of the centre bracket (for the split seat). Also check there is no knocking

Move on to Step 4

No plastic bush on the rotation pin

Remove the backrest and fit the plastic noise prevention bush

4

CHECK ON CORRECT FASTENING OF CENTRE BRACKET

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the fastening of the centre bracket with the backrest structure and with the floor is correct

Move on to Step 5

Incorrect fastening of bracket with backrest and/or with floor

Retighten the centre bracket to the backrest and/or the floor to the correct torque

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

The fault persists

Replace the left backrest

Op. 7050A13 SPLIT REAR SEAT LEFT BACKREST - R.R.

Replace the right backrest

Op. 7050A15 SPLIT REAR SEAT RIGHT BACKREST - R.R.
