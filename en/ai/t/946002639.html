Op. 7040H50 REAR PANEL (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R

1. Working on the inner door panel, remove the damper seal (1a) detaching the adhesive surface (1c).

2. Release the retaining clips.

3. Remove the frame (3a) releasing the retainer (3b) and keep the grille (3c).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
