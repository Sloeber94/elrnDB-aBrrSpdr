Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods; only with equipment: Gas discharge headlamps

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Turn on the diagnostic tester and go to Instruments.

In Voltmeter mode, select a full scale deflection value of 20 V and a time base of 2 s.

Connect the voltmeter red probe to Pin 10 of the master headlamp 14-way connector

See F010 LEFT HEADLAMP

and the red probe to earth.

Turn one wheel by hand and when the speed is 4-6 km/h press the START button.

Check that the signal acquired looks the attached graph without any gaps. The test is complete

Move on to Step 2

Speed signal

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

2

Check for wiring continuity between Pin 28 of Body Computer connector B

See M001 BODY COMPUTER

, and Pin 10 of the Master headlamp 14-way connector

See F010 LEFT HEADLAMP

The test can be considered over

3

Check for wheel speed signal errors in the ABS control unit

Continue as instructed by the diagnostic equipment

4

Check for wiring continuity between the ABS control unit and the Body Computer

See E7022 VDC

Replace Body Computer

Op. 5505A32 BODY COMPUTER - R.R

5

Check that the fault is no longer present

Replace the Master headlamp

Op. 5540B81 LEFT GAS DISCHARGE LIGHT CLUSTER - R.R.
