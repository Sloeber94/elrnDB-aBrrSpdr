Removing

Refitting

Op. 7045A43 FRONT SEAT EXTERIOR GUARD (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R

1. Cut control cable (1a) and release wiring (1b).

2. Detach the electrical connection.

Description

Component

DRIVER SEAT BACKREST ADJUSTMENT MOTOR

See N132 DRIVER'S SEAT BACKREST ADJUSTMENT MOTOR

PASSENGER SEAT BACKREST ADJUSTMENT MOTOR

See N135 PASSENGER SEAT BACKREST ADJUSTMENT MOTOR

3. Release the circlip.

4. Remove the retaining band.

5. Remove the motor.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Electric seats

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the motor is not damaged.

- Position the motor in its housing.

- Fit the retaining band in position.

- Attach the circlip.

- Attach the electrical connection.

- Position the wiring and fit the retaining band.

Op. 7045A43 FRONT SEAT EXTERIOR GUARD (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Op. 7045A10 FRONT SEAT (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

1. Detach the electrical connection for the motor.

Description

Component

DRIVER SEAT BACKREST ADJUSTMENT MOTOR

See N132 DRIVER'S SEAT BACKREST ADJUSTMENT MOTOR

PASSENGER SEAT BACKREST ADJUSTMENT MOTOR

See N135 PASSENGER SEAT BACKREST ADJUSTMENT MOTOR

2. First release motor (2a) from retainer (2b) then from retainer (2c).

3. Disconnect motor (2a) from joint (3a) of flexible shaft (3b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the backrest adjustment motor is not damaged.

- Connect the motor to the joint of the flexible shaft.

- Attach the motor with flexible shaft to the retainers.

- Secure the electrical connection for the motor.

Op. 7045A10 FRONT SEAT (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

- Check that the backrest moves correctly.
