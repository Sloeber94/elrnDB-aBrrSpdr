Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Connect Nav+

DESCRIPTION

A road map navigation system reveals the vehicle's position on a map (showing town streets and main roads/motorways) at every moment and pinpoints it on the CONNECT display.

In this way the driver is guided step-by-step to the required destination, by means of voice messages and visual information. The system also provides additional information for reaching general services, namely: hotels, car parks, restaurants, chemists, service stations, hospitals, ALFA ROMEO service outlets, railway stations, airports etc.

The navigation system uses GPS (Global Positioning System) satellite localisation technology and is fitted, when ordered, in the integrated radio-phone-navigation system known as CONNECT NAV +. The navigation CD-ROM drive is located in the unit.

The aerial has two functions (GSM/GPS) for the operation of the telephone and for the operation of the navigator and it is installed on the roof or, on versions without a telephone, it only has a GPS function and is concealed in the roof trim.

Vehicle position is identified by analysing GPS data together with speedometer and reverse gear signals received from the vehicle.

Some versions are equipped with a series of buttons on the steering wheel: these are used to send out radio and also phone commands.

Other control buttons are located in the front courtesy light.
