Test results

1

CHECK ON INSTALLATION OF ALIGNMENT SENSORS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the condition and correct installation of the front and rear attitude sensors. Ensuere that the bracket and rod are secured correctly for both front and rear sensors

Move on to Step 2

Mechanical installation of front sensor (brackets, connecting rods) incorrect

Refit front sensor correctly

Op. 5540C42 SENSOR FOR CORRECTING/ALIGNING GAS DISCHARGE LIGHTING UNITS ON FRONT SUSPENSION - R.R.

Mechanical installation of rear sensor (brackets, connecting rods) incorrect

Refit rear sensor correctly

Op. 5540C46 SENSOR FOR CORRECTING/ALIGNING GAS DISCHARGE LIGHTING UNITS ON REAR SUSPENSION - R.R.

2

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that there are no errors by connecting to the master headlamp (left side) control unit.

Move on to Step 3

Presence of error B1020 detected - Front sensor signal

Move on to Step 3

Presence of error B1021 detected - Rear sensor signal

Move on to Step 3

Presence of error B1022 detected - Sensor supply

Move on to Step 3

Presence of error B1040 detected - Headlamp alignment corrector

Move on to Step 5

3

CHECK ON ALIGNMENT SENSOR SUPPLY

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the attitude sensors are receiving a correct supply by carrying out the test

See Test 5540BB CHECK ALIGNMENT SENSORS

Move on to Step 4

4

CHECK ON CONNECTIONS INSIDE THE HEADLAMPS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the connections inside the headlamps for the vertical adjustment motors are correctly connected

See E3080 HEADLAMP AGLINMENT CORRECTOR

Move on to Step 5

Connector for right headlamp motor not correctly fitted

Fit connector for right headlamp

Connector for left headlamp motor not correctly fitted

Fit connector for left headlamp

5

CHECK ON OPERATION OF HEADLAMP ALIGNMENT ACTUATORS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Replace the left headlamps

Op. 5540B81 LEFT GAS DISCHARGE LIGHT CLUSTER - R.R.

and right

Op. 5540B82 RIGHT GAS DISCHARGE LIGHT CLUSTER - R.R.

and check that the motors are working properly

End of diagnosis

Right headlamp alignment corrector not working

Move on to Step 6

Left headlamp alignment corrector not working

Move on to Step 6

6

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Refit the headlamp removed previously and replace the ballast control unit

Op. 5540B40 CONTROL UNIT IN LEFT GAS DISCHARGE LIGHT CLUSTER - R.R.

and/or

Op. 5540B41 CONTROL UNIT IN RIGHT GAS DISCHARGE LIGHT CLUSTER - R.R.

End of diagnosis
