FUNCTIONAL DESCRIPTION

The services relay T13, located in the junction unit under the dashboard B2, supplies the sun roof control motor M185 - pin 3 - via the line protected by fuse F46 of B2 by means of the strategy described above and controlled by the Body Computer M1.

Pin 6 of M185 receives an ignition-operated power supply (INT) from a line protected by fuse F49 in under-dashboard control unit B2.

Pins 1 and 2 of M185 are earthed, while pins 5 and 10 receive opening and closure signals from switches on courtesy light G10.
