STEP

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

REMEDIAL ACTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Check on +30 control unit power supply

Connect using the Examiner in Voltmeter mode to the positive probe at pin 4 of connector and the negative probe earthed.

The direct voltage reading should be around 12 Volts

See E3096 HOOD OPENING

Connect the positive probe to pin 2 of connector B and the negative to earth.

The direct voltage reading should be around 12 Volts

See E3096 HOOD OPENING

Replace the 30 A fuse F62 in the luggage comaprtment fuse box after having identified the cause of the malfunction.

Replace the 20 A fuse F63 in the luggage comaprtment fuse box after having identified the cause of the malfunction.

2

Check earth connection

Disconnect connector B of the control unit

Check that the resistance between pins 1 and 3 and earth is less than 1 Ohm.

Restore the correct earth connection.
