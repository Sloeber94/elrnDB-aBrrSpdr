P030 NO DOORS / BOOT OPEN BUZZER SIGNAL WITH CAR IN MOTION

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the vehicle moving, the accidental opening of one of the doors or the boot lid is not signalled by the buzzer. The instrument panel node acquires the door or boot opening switch status and vehicle speed signal from the network. If the door or the boot is opened and the vehicle speed is above 4 km/h, an acoustic warning is issued.
