STEP

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

REMEDIAL ACTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Check on the operation of the electric pump

Using the Examiner voltmeter connect to the pump terminals, press the hood control button and check that there is a voltage of around 12 Volts

Check the continuity of the wiring between hood control unit and the electric pump.

Replace the 30 A fuse F62 in the luggage comaprtment fuse box after having identified the cause of the malfunction.

Replace the hood control unit

Op. 7030H50 AUTOMATIC HOOD ACTIVATING SYSTEM CONTROL UNIT - R.R. FOLLOWING SYSTEM CHECK

Replace the hydraulic unit

Op. 7030H58 AUTOMATIC HOOD HYDRAULIC ACTIVATING SYSTEM MOTOR/PUMP ASSEMBLY - R.R.
