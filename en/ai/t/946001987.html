1084A16 OIL DIPSTICK PIPE - R.R

Removing

Refitting

1. Remove the engine oil dipstick.

2. Undo the bolt (2a) and remove the engine oil dipstick tube (2b).

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the engine oil dipstick pipe back in its housing and fasten it with the bolt.

- Place the engine oil dipstick back in its housing.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Undo the bolt (1a) and remove the engine oil dipstick (1b) from its housing.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the engine oil dipstick pipe back in its housing and fasten it with the bolt.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

Op. 7055B54 UNDER ENGINE PROTECTION/GUARD - R.R.

1. Undo the bolt fixing the engine oil dipstick pipe to the engine crankcase sump.

2. Undo the upper bolt (2a) and remove the engine oil dipstick tube (2b) complete with rod (2c).

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the engine oil dipstick pipe complete with rod back in its housing and fasten it with the upper bolt.

- Tighten the bolt fixing the engine oil dipstick pipe to the engine crankcase sump.

Op. 7055B54 UNDER ENGINE PROTECTION/GUARD - R.R.

- Remove the vehicle from the lift.
