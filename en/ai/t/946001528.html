Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The rear fog lamps are controlled and diagnosed directly by the Body Computer Node.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Connect the Examiner and check the following at the Body Computer Node:

- that there are no errors;

- the plausibility of the Rear Fog Lamps On/Off parameter.

Move on to Step 2

Presence of errors.

Proceed as instructed by the diagnostic tester

The fog lights on/off parameter does not change

Move on to Step 5

2

CHECK ON OPERATION OF REAR FOG LAMPS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the rear fog lamps are working properly and check the operation of the wiring

Move on to Step 3

Lamp not working

Replace the rear fog lamp

Op. 5540A50 BULB FOR REAR LIGHT CLUSTER (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - REPLACE

3

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Keeping the diagnostic equipment connected, check in active diagnosis that the rear fog lamps are working properly

Move on to Step 5

Rear fog lamps not working

Move on to Step 4

4

BODY COMPUTER CONNECTION CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check for deformed terminals or connections not present on the Body Computer

Move on to Step 6

Missing connections/pins bent

Renew the correct connections

5

REAR FOG LAMPS ON COMMAND CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the reversing switch is working properly

Move on to Step 6

Reversing light on/off button faulty

Replace the control panel

Op. 7040A56 SWITCH UNIT IN DASHBOARD - R.R.

6

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

The fault persists

Replace Body Computer

Op. 5505A32 BODY COMPUTER - R.R
