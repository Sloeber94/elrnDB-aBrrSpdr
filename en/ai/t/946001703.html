Test results

1

CHECK ON CORRECT TIGHTENING CYCLE

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Loosen the bolts securing the seat to the floor (4 M10 bolts) and carry out the correct tightening cycle

Move on to Step 2

Regular slidings

Retighten the seat following the correct fitting cycle

2

CHECK FOR PRESENCE OF INTERFERENCE

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Remove the seat and check for any interference with the carpet or sound insulation under the guides

Move on to Step 4

Interference under the guides

Remove the interference and retighten the seat following the correct cycle

3

CHECK ON FLATNESS OF BODYSHELL BACKREST FOR SECURING SEAT

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the bodyshell bracket for securing the seat is properly flat

Move on to Step 4

Lack of planarity between internal and external brackets

Place a correct size shim between the guide and the bracket

4

CHECK ON GUIDE SLIDING

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the guides slide correctly

Move on to Step 5

Guide sliding regular

Replace the guide release mounting (distorted)

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

The fault persists

Replace the guides
