U003 EXHAUST GAS ODOURS FROM THE BOOT

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods

When the vehicle is moving there is a smell of exhaust gases coming from the rear area

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods

1

AIR OUTLET VENTS CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the rear air outlet vents. In particular, check:

- the positioning of the fins (they should open outwards)

- the correct fitting

Move on to Step 2

Problems with the fitting

Restore the correct positioning of the vents

Op. 5010C60 LH OR RH REAR AIR OUTLET VENT (ONE) - R.R

2

TAILGATE SEAL CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the condition of the tailgate perimeter seal (no creases or wrinkles)

Move on to Step 3

Perimeter seal not correctly fitted.

Refit correctly. If necesssary, replace the seal

Op. 7015A30 TAILGATE COMPARTMENT SEAL - R+R

Perimeter seal faulty

Replace the seal

Op. 7015A30 TAILGATE COMPARTMENT SEAL - R+R

3

TAILGATE ALIGNMENT CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the fitting of the tailgate

In particular, make sure the alignment of the tailgate is correct checking that the seal is in contact along the entire perimeter

Move on to Step 4

Tailgate fitting faulty

Restore the alignment of the tailgate

Op. 7015A14 TAILGATE - ADJUST CENTRING AND PROFILE - INCLUDES HINGE AND COMPARTMENT SEAL ADAPTATION

4

BOOT HOUSING SEAL CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the boot housing seal is correct

Move on to Step 5

Boot housing seal faulty

Check that the boot housing seal is correct

5

UNDERBODY CHECKS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the underbody plugs are present, in the area of the boot

End of diagnosis

No underbody plugs

Fit the missing plugs
