Removing

Refitting

- Prepare the hood for the operation.

Op. 0010T79 HOOD HYDRAULIC SYSTEM - DISCHARGE AND RESTORE PRESSURE

- Open the boot lid.

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and the nuts (1b) and remove the hinge (1c) for the hood housing compartment cover.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the hinge is not damaged.

- Position the hood housing compartment cover hinge.

- Screw in the nuts and bolts without tightening (for the subsequent adjustment).

- Close the boot lid.

- If discharged, restore the hood hydraulic system pressure.

Op. 7030H03 HOOD MANUAL OPENING FOLLOWING DEACTIVATION OF HYDRAULIC SYSTEM - CARRY OUT PROCEDURE

- Check the alignment of the hood housing compartment cover with the vehicle bodywork.

Op. 7025D10 HOOD HOUSING COMPARTMENT COVER - ALIGNMENT
