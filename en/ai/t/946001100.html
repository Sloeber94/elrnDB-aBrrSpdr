Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods; only with equipment: Gas discharge headlamps

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Remove the headlamp. Remove the headlamp bulb

Op. 5540B45 GAS DISCHARGE DIPPED/MAIN BEAM HEADLAMP BULB - R.R.

Check that the 3-way connection (with two wires) supplying the headlamp high beam control coil is correctly inserted

Connect the electrical connection

See E2012 MAIN BEAM HEADLAMPS

2

Check the headlamp high beam function is operating correctly.

The test can be considered over

Replace the left headlamp

Op. 5540B81 LEFT GAS DISCHARGE LIGHT CLUSTER - R.R.

or right headlamp

Op. 5540B82 RIGHT GAS DISCHARGE LIGHT CLUSTER - R.R.

(the faulty unit) and move on to Step 3

3

Check the headlamp high beam function is operating correctly.

Refit the headlamp replaced previously and replace the control unit]

Op. 5540B43 CONTROL UNIT FOR ONE RIGHT GAS DISCHARGE LIGHT CLUSTER ONE LH OR RH - R.R. WITH LIGHT CLUSTER REMOVED

A - Bulb connector

B - Coil power connection for high beam function control
