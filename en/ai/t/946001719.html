Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the vehicle moving, there is noise coming from the rear area of the passenger compartment

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

First of all check that the boot is correctly shut.

Check that the wind noise is actually coming from the rear of the passenger compartment

Move on to Step 1

Boot not correctly shut

Close the boot correctly. If the problem persists, move on to Step 0

The wind noise is coming from the doors

Proceed as instructed in the

See table T001 WIND NOISE FROM THE FRONT DOORS

The wind noise is coming from the area of the bonnet

Proceed as instructed in the

See table T005 BONNET WIND NOISE

The wind noise is coming from the area of the windscreen.

Proceed as instructed in the

See table T006 WINDSCREEN WIND NOISE

The wind noise is coming from the lower part of the passenger compartment

Proceed as instructed in the

See table T010 PASSENGER COMPARTMENT WIND NOISE, LOWER AREA (UNDERBODY)

1

REAR LIGHTS CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the rear lights. In particular, check:

- the alignment of the lights with the wing or the tailgate is correct

- the lights are correctly fitted

Move on to Step 2

Fitting of lights faulty

Refit correctly

Op. 5540A44 REAR LIGHT CLUSTER (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

2

TAILGATE CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the condition of the tailgate perimeter seal (no creases or wrinkles)

End of diagnosis

Perimeter seal not correctly fitted.

Refit correctly.

If necesssary, replace the seal

Op. 7015A30 TAILGATE COMPARTMENT SEAL - R+R

Perimeter seal faulty

Replace the seal

Op. 7015A30 TAILGATE COMPARTMENT SEAL - R+R
