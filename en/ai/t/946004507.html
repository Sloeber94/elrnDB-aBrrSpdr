C022 THE GEARBOX IS STUCK IN A SPECIFIC GEAR

Path

Kind
fault diagnosis table (symptom -> numbered test steps)
Applies to

Interactive page

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS; all production periods

The vehicle remains with the gearbox locked in one gear when at a standstill and when the engine is accelerated the rpm increases (the gear engaged appears on the display)

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS; all production periods

Test results

1

BATTERY VOLTAGE CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check battery status (no less than 11 Volts)

Check the state of the connections and the wiring continuity between the engine compartment control unit and the gearbox control unit

Check that the gearbox control unit is receiving the correct power supply

See E7051 ROBOTIZED GEARBOX

Move on to Step 2

Battery voltage less than 11 Volts

Recharge/replace the battery if defective

Gearbox control unit power supply wiring defective

Restore correct power supply

Gearbox control unit earth connection defective.

Restore earth connection
