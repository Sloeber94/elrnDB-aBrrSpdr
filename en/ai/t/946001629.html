If an obstacle located between the glass and the upper outer casing whilst it is closing, the glass does not stop immediately or change direction.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The window system features a short drop function to ensure complete closure of the door lacking an upper reinforcement. For more information, seeSee descriptions 7005 PASSENGER COMPARTMENT FRONT SIDE DOORS

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

If an obstacle is detected the change of direction stage lasts around 300 ms.
