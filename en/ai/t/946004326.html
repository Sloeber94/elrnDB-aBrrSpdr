Functional description

The robotized gearbox control unit M54 controls and regulates the entire gearbox management system

The direct power supply for the control unit arrives from the battery from the line for fuse F18 of the engine compartment junction unit B1, at pin 3 of connector A of M54.

The direct power supply for the pump is protected by the line for the specific maxifuse F8 of B1 at pin 5 of connector A of M54.

The ignition-controlled supply (15/54) arrives from the line for fuse F24 of B1 at pin 10 of connector A of M54.

A group of sensors on the gearbox inform the control unit of the gear engaged.

The potentiometers receive a power supply from pin 4 of connector B; pin 6 provides the reference earth: the gear selection potentiometer K68 returns a signal to pin 5 of connector B; the gear engagement potentiometer K69 returns a signal to pin 12 of connector B.

The clutch position potentiometer K95 is supplied by pin 11 of connector B, receives a reference earth from pin 10, returns a signal to pin 9 of connector B of M54

Pins 3 and 8 of connector B M54 are connected to the vehicle speed sensor (gearbox output) K78 .

Gear control unit H37 - connector A - is connected to control unit M54 to indicate the gear engagement request: these signals are sent to pins 12 (UP), 16 (DOWN), 11 (TIP) and 7 (N) of connector A of M54; whilst connector A receives a reference earth from pin 13.

This earth is sent to the controls on the steering wheel - clock spring D47 - which relays a signal to pin 6 of connector A of M54.

The supply for gear control unit H37 - connector A - arrives from the ignition-controlled line (15/54) protected by fuse F24 of engine compartment junction unit B1.

The button for selecting the SPORT operating mode - H37 - connector B - is connected to control unit M54 at pin 17 of connector A and is lit up by the ignition-controlled line (INT) protected by fuse F35 of the junction unit under the dashboard B2.

Pin 9 of connector A of M54 receives the signal coming from the brake lights switch I30 with an ignition-controlled supply (INT) protected by fuse F37 of junction unit B2.

Control unit M54 also receives the driver's front door opening signal, which is designed to activate the pump to pressureize the circuit; the signal from lock N50 reaches pin 2 of connector A of M54.

On the basis of the operating logic the control unit controls the electro-hydraulic unit located in the gearbox which produces the gear changes: the electric pump N47 which keeps the oil in the hydraulic unit pressurized is controlled from pin 1 of connector B.

The control unit then controls the opening of the hydraulic unit solenoid valves, connected by dedicated internal wiring.

Control unit M54 controls the SHIFT-LOCK electromagnet from pin 18 of connector A, H37 pins 7 and 12 of connector A - controlled via relay T20 of engine compartment junction unit B1.

Pin 8 of connector A of control unit M54 is connected directly to pin B4 of the steering lock control unit M89. If the gear lever is not in N, the signal sent inhibits starting, whilst a "starting" signal reaches pin 1 of connector A of control unit M54 from pin C3 of the steering lock control unit M89.

See E5010 STARTING AND RECHARING

When reverse gear is engaged, control unit M54 sends a power supply to the reversing light from pin 4 of connector A

See E2022 REVERSING LIGHTS

Signals are sent, via the CAN - pins 19-20 and 22-23 of connector A of M54 - for the exchange of information with the engine management control unit M10 (wheel speed, brake pedal pressed/released, engine rpm signal, engine torque variation request) and with the instrument panel E50 to control the "gearbox failure" warning light and to display the gear engaged as well as signals for activating the buzzer.

See E1050 CAN CONNECTION LINES

The autodiagnostic data can be read by connecting to connector C of the Body Computer M1 - pin 7: it receives the signals from pin 21 of connector A of M54. (the line is shared with engine control unit M10 via the tester line

See E8010 DIAGNOSTIC MULTIPLE CONNECTOR
