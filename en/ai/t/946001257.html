Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The body marking is stamped on the passenger compartment floor panel, near the right front seat, and includes:

- Vehicle type: ZAR 939000

- Vehicle manufacture serial number (chassis number).

Access is gained by lifting the special window in the carpet.
