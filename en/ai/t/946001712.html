2

REAR PARCEL SHELF CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check:

- the correct fastening of the rear parcel shelf

- the presence of the anti-noise rubber mountings

- that there is no interference between the rear parcel shelf bracket and rear backrest

Move on to Step 3

Fastening faulty

Renew the correct fastening

Anti-noise rubber mountings missing

Refit the anti-noise rubber mountings

Interference between the rear parcel shelf bracket and rear backrest

Eliminate the interference, fitting the faulty components correctly

3

SEAT BELT MOUNTINGS CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the correct fastening of the rear seat belts

Move on to Step 4

Fastening faulty

Renew the correct fastening

Op. 7065B14 ANCHORAGE ROD FOR (ONE) REAR SEAT BELT LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

4

REAR PILLAR COVER CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Remove the rear pillar covers

Op. 7040H42 REAR PILLAR TRIM (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT- R.R

and check for the presence of the felt pads

Move on to Step 5

Felt pads missing

Restore the felt pads

Op. 7040H42 REAR PILLAR TRIM (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT- R.R
