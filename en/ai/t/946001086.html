Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Check that the alternator belt is correctly fitted and correctly tensioned

Restore the correct tension. If necesssary, replace the belt

Op. 1092G10 SINGLE ENGINE BELT - R.R

2

Check that the alternator electrical connections do not contain insulation/continuity errors

See E5030 PETROL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Restore the correct connections. Replace the wiring if necessary

3

Replace the pressure regulator

Op. 5530A24 ALTERNATOR VOLTAGE REGULATOR - R.R. WITH ALTERNATOR REMOVED
