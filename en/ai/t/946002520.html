Op. 7005E11 LH OR RH FRONT DOOR INNER PANEL - R+R WITH FOULING PARTS REMOVED

1. Working on the inner door panel, remove the bolts fixing the oddments pocket.

2. Release the ends (2a) and (2b) of the spring (2c).

3. Release the retainer (3a) for the flap end of travel rod rod (3b) for the pocket.

4. Extract the flap rotation pins.

5. Remove the oddments pocket (5a) and keep the flap (5b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the oddments pocket is not damaged.

- Place the oddments pocket and flap in position.

- Fit the flap rotation pins.

- Fit the pocket flap end of travel rod in place and attach the retainer.

- Attach the ends of the spring.

- Tighten the pockect fixing bolts.
