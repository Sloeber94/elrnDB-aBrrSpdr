1. Undo clips (1a) to release flexible gear selector and engagement rods (1b) from the gearbox manual control lever support.

2. Undo bolts (2a) and lift up gearbox manual control lever support (2b) as necessary and disconnect flexible gear selector and engagement rods (2c).

3. Remove the gearbox manual control lever.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Connect the flexible gear selection and engagement rods, place the manual gear control lever support in its housing and tighten the bolts to the recommended torque.
