Op. 0010T20 ENGINE COOLANT - CHANGE

1. Loosen the band and disconnect the rigid pipe from the thermostat to the heat exchanger, heat exchanger side.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods

1. Open the starter motor supply lead retaining bands.

2. Undo the bolt fixing the rigid pipe from the thermostat to the heat exchanger to the engine block.

3. Retract the rigid pipe from the thermostat to the heat exchanger, thermostat side until it is disconnected and then remove it.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods

- Remove the O-ring.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit a new O-ring and connect the rigid pipe from the thermostat to the heat exchanger, thermostat side.

- Tighten the bolt fixing the rigid pipe from the thermostat to the heat exchanger to the engine block.

- Connect the rigid pipe from the thermostat to the heat exchanger, heat exchanger side and tighten the band.

- Position the starter motor supply lead and tighten the retaining clips.
