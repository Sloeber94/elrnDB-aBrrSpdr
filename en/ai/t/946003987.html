Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods

To check the timing system setting, fit the appropriate templates as described below.

- Remove the spark plugs referring to

Op. 5510D10 SPARK PLUGS - R+R

Removal of all the spark plugs is necessary to facilitate crankshaft rotation required at the next stage.

1. Fit tool (1a) complete with dial gauge (1b) in the seat for the spark plug for cylinder no. 1.

T.D.C. check

3.2 JTS

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods

- Adjust the service pulley nut to turn the crankshaft clockwise, timing side, until the piston for cylinder no.1 is at T.D.C. in the combustion stroke.

If TDC is exceeded, do not turn backwards but proceed with the rotation in the same direction, rotating the crankshaft through another two revolutions, then return the piston for cylinder no. 1 to TDC.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods

1. Position template (1a) on the left cylinder head camshaft making sure that the wording "ALTO - UP" (1b) is uppermost.
