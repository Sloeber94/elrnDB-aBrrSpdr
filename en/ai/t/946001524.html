CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment to the diagnostic socket and check the following at the Body Computer Node:

- that there are no errors

- the plausibility of the direction indicators on/off parameter

- check the operation of the direction indicator bulbs through active diagnosis

Move on to Step 4

Presence of errors

Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment.

Parameter does not change status

Move on to Step 3

The bulbs do not come on

Move on to Step 4

2

ELECTRICAL SYSTEM CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the continuity of the wiring between the exterior lights control and the Body Computer Node

See E2020 DIRECTION INDICATORS / HAZARD WARNING LIGHTS

Move on to Step 3

Wiring broken

Renew the correct electrical connections

See E2020 DIRECTION INDICATORS / HAZARD WARNING LIGHTS
