Removing

Refitting

Op. 7040B22 LUGGAGE COMPARTMENT/LOAD PLATFORM MOULDING - R+R

1. Undo the bolts.

2. Move the striker (2a) and disconnect the emergency opening rod (2b) releasing it from the retainers (2c) and (2d).

3. Keep the striker.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the striker is not damaged.

- Connect the emergency opening rod to the striker retainers.

1. Place the striker in position and screw in the bolts without tightening them.

2. Adjust the position of the striker; the arrows show the possible movements.

The striker can even be adjusted after the moulding is refitted.

3. Tighten the bolts.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods
