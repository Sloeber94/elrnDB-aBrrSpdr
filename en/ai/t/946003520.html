The diagnostic equipment signals: Error P2293 (fuel pressure regulator (high pressure pump inlet or pipe).

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

This error is detected if the control unit recognizes a mechanical malfunction of the pressure regulator on the rail by means of the internal evaluation logic.
