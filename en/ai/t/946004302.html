Removing

Refitting

Get the plug kit from the Parts Dept. to be used for the open parts during the following procedure.

- Place the gear lever in N.

- Connect an Examiner and run the depressurization procedure.

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

Op. 5530B10 BATTERY - R+R

Op. 5530B52 BATTERY SUPPORT/DRIP TRAY - R.R

Op. 7055B54 UNDER ENGINE PROTECTION/GUARD - R.R.

1. Undo the cap and draw off the fluid from the reservoir using a suitable syringe.

2. Disconnect the pipe from the hydraulic unit to the reservoir, reservoir side.

3. Disconnect the pipe from the hydraulic unit to the reservoir (3a) from the angular connector (3b) and remove it.

Collect the fluid that comes out in a suitable container.

Proceed very carefully when removing the pipe in order not to damage the angular connector.

Replace the pipe each time it is removed.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS; all production periods

Removing

- Connect a new pipe from the hydraulic unit to the reservoir, angular connector side.

- Connect the pipe from the hydraulic unit to the reservoir, reservoir side.
