Removing

Refitting

1. Disconnect the electrical connection.

Description

Component

CLUTCH PEDAL SWITCH

See I031 CLUTCH PEDAL SWITCH

- Rotate the switch on the clutch pedal (2a) through about 90° in an anti-clockwise and extract it from the mounting (2b).

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the switch is not damaged.

- Gently lower the clutch pedal and fit the switch in place, then rotate it through about 90° in a clockwise direction until it is locked.

- Connect the electrical connection.
