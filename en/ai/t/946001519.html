I004 SIDE LIGHTS / NUMBER PLATE LIGHT AND DIPPED BEAMS DO NOT WORK IN AUTO MODE

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The side / no. plate lights and dipped headlamps are not working with the exterior lights stalk switch in the AUTO position N.B: with the AUTO function, the side lights/number plate lights and low beams are turned on automatically by the Body Computer Node, which receives a command from the dusk sensor (CPS)

The sensitivity of the CPS may be adjusted by means of the Menu Esc control on the left panel

If there is a fault with the dusk sensor and the C.L.E. in AUTO mode the Body Computer Node switches on the Side / No. Plate lights and the Dipped Headlamps in any case.
