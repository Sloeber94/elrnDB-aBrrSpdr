STEP

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Check whether error P2455 - Differential pressure sensor - is present using the diagnostic equipment

Replace differential pressure sensor

Op. 1080B82 PARTICULATE FILTER DIFFERENTIAL PRESSURE (D.P.F.) SENSOR - R.R.

2

Press the START/STOP button and turn off the engine.

Disconnect the wiring harness connector from the FAP differential pressure sensor.

Press the START/STOP button.

Check that the voltage across pin 1 and 2 of the sensor

See K187 FAP DIFFERENTIAL PRESSURE SENSOR

is greater than 4.8 V.

Move on to Step 4

Move on to Step 3

3

If faults are found, restore the wiring between the engine control unit and the downstream temperature sensor

See E5070 PARTICLE FILTER SYSTEM

Replace the wiring if necessary

The test can be considered over

Replace the engine management control unit

Op. 1060G80 DIESEL ELECTRONIC INJECTION CONTROL UNIT - R.R

4

On the diagnostic tester, check whether the differential pressure sensor voltage parameter is between 4.8 and 5.2 V

Move on to Step 3

5

Connect a shunt cable with a fuse between sensor Pin 3

See K187 FAP DIFFERENTIAL PRESSURE SENSOR

and the earth.

On the diagnostic tester, check whether the differential pressure sensor voltage parameter is less than 0.3 V

Move on to Step 3

6

Replace differential pressure sensor

Op. 1080B82 PARTICULATE FILTER DIFFERENTIAL PRESSURE (D.P.F.) SENSOR - R.R.
