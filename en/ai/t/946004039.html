With the vehicle moving, there is noise coming from the hood with the hood closed.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods; only with equipment: Spider

Test results

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the wind noise is actually coming from the hood.

Move on to Step 1

The wind noise is coming from the doors.

Proceed as instructed

See table T001 WIND NOISE FROM THE FRONT DOORS

The wind noise is coming from the area of the bonnet

Proceed as instructed

See table T005 BONNET WIND NOISE

The wind noise is coming from the area of the windscreen.

Proceed as instructed

See table T006 WINDSCREEN WIND NOISE

The wind noise is coming from the lower part of the passenger compartment.

Proceed as instructed

See table T010 PASSENGER COMPARTMENT WIND NOISE, LOWER AREA (UNDERBODY)

The wind noise is coming from the main hoop rear seal.

Refit the main hoop rear seal properly.

1

CORRECT WINDOW CLOSING CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check whether the wind noise is coming from the windows, not closed properly after the hood has been closed.

Move on to Step 2

Windows not closing properly.

Restore the correct window adjustment

Op. 7005D11 LOWERING WINDOW (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT FOR FRONT SIDE DOOR - CHECK SLIDING - INCLUDES POSITIONING GUIDES, POSITIONING AND FASTENING WINDOW SUPPORT TO CONTROL

2

HOOD FRONT SEAL CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the condition of the hood front seal and make sure it is correctly matched with the hood.

Move on to Step 3

Seal fitting faulty.

Refit the seal correctly

Op. 7030H35 HOOD FRONT SEAL - R.R.

3

DOOR HOUSING SEAL CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the condition of the door housing seals and make sure the three seals are correctly matched.

Move on to Step 4

Seal fitting faulty.

Refit the seals correctly

Op. 7030H37 SEAL (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT, HOOD SIDE SEAL - R.R.

4

HOOD REAR SEAL CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the condition of the hood rear seal and make sure it matches with the left/right fixed seal on the hood cloth.

Move on to Step 5

Seal fitting faulty.

Refit the seal correctly

Op. 7030H39 HOOD REAR SEAL - R.R.

5

CHECK STITCHED SEAL ON FABRIC

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check for the presence of pinches on the stitched seal of the fabric with the seal strip due to incorrect hood closing/opening cycles

Move on to Step 6

Pinches between the stitched seal on the fabric and the strip seal.

Re-do the complete hood opening/closing cycle to eliminate pinches in the seal.

6

MIRROR AREA CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the wind noise is not coming from the mirror area, making sure that the door housing seal and the windscreen pillar are correctly matched.

End of diagnosis

Incorrect matching between door housing seal and windscreen pillar seal.

Restore the correct matching of the door housing seal and the windscreen pillar seal.
