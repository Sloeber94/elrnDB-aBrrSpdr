The diagnostic equipment signals: Error P0697 (Sensor power supply 3).

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

This error is detected if there is a short circuit to positive (detail "beyond the upper limit") or a short circuit to earth or a break (detail "below the lower limit") in the circuit connected to Pin 22, or 44 of the junction unit.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

1

Junction unit connections check

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the engine management control unit connections are correct by carrying out the test

See Test 1060GP CHECK ENGINE CONTROL UNIT CONNECTIONS

End of diagnosis
