Op. 7050A13 SPLIT REAR SEAT LEFT BACKREST - R.R.

Op. 7040H50 REAR PANEL (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R

- Proceed as follows to remove the front trim.

1. Undo the retainers.

2. Remove the front trim.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- Proceed as follows to remove the rear trim.

Op. 7040H54 GRILLE FOR SPEAKER IN REAR SIDE PANEL (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

1. Undo the bolts.

2. Undo the retainers.

3. Remove the rear trim.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Proceed as follows to refit the front trim.

- Check that the front trim is not damaged.

- Position the front trim and attach the retainers.

- Proceed as follows to refit the rear trim.

- Check that the rear trim is not damaged.

- Position the rear trim and attach the retainers.

- Tighten the bolts.

Op. 7040H54 GRILLE FOR SPEAKER IN REAR SIDE PANEL (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

- Refit the panel.
