1. Undo the bolts fixing the lower reaction rod for the power unit, engine crankcase sump side.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts fixing the power unit rear support flexible mounting, rigid support side.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the rigid power unit rear support (1b).

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS; all production periods
