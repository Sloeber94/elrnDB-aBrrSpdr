J007 WINDSCREEN WASHER AND REAR WASHER DO NO WORK

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The windscreen washer and rearscreen washer do not work properly when the lever in the steering column switch unit is operated.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The windscreen/rearscreen washer pump motor is the "two-way" type; depending on the command, set through the special lever on the steering column switch unit, the motor covers the windscreen or rearscreen washing funciton.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
