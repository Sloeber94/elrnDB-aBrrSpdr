The diagnostic equipment signals: Error P0204 (Injector for cylinder 4)

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

This error is detected during the diagnosis of the junction unit final stage (break in the circuit). In this case, the defective injector is no longer activated, whilst the remaining injectors work as normal.
