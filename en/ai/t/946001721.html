U001 FUEL ODOURS FROM THE BOOT

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods

When the vehicle is moving there is a smell of fuel, especially after refilling, coming from the rear area.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

First of all, check that there are no leaks from or damage to the fuel tank

Move on to Step 1

Leaks from the fuel tank

Restore the correct seal for the fuel tank. If necessary, replace it

Op. 1040A14 FUEL TANK - R + R FOR REPLACEMENT

1

AIR OUTLET VENTS CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the rear air outlet vents.

In particular, check:

- the positioning of the fins (they should open outwards)

- the correct fitting

Move on to Step 2

Problems with the fitting

Restore the correct positioning of the vents

Op. 5010C60 LH OR RH REAR AIR OUTLET VENT (ONE) - R.R

2

TANK BREATHER CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the fuel tank breather is correctly positioned

Move on to Step 3

Breather position incorrect

Restore the correct position of the breather

3

TANK FILLER CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the tank filler. More specifically:

- seal of the filler housing in the wing

- condition of the filler

- seal of the cap

Move on to Step 4

Filler faulty

Renew the filler. If necessary, replace it

Op. 1040A30 FUEL FILLER PIPE ASSEMBLY- R + R

Cap faulty

Replace the cap

Op. 1040A40 PLUG FOR FUEL FILLER PIPE - R + R

4

FUEL PUMP COVER CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the seal of the fuel pump cover

Move on to Step 5

Seal worn

Replace the seal

Op. 1040A70 SUBMERGED PUMP ASSEMBLY COMPLETE WITH LEVEL GAUGE CONTROL - R + R

Cover damaged

Replace the cover

Op. 1040A70 SUBMERGED PUMP ASSEMBLY COMPLETE WITH LEVEL GAUGE CONTROL - R + R

5

FUEL VAPOUR RECOVERY SYSTEM CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the seal of the fuel vapour recovery system

Move on to Step 6

Leaks from the fuel vapour recovery system

Tighten the connectors and bands. Replace any faulty components, if necessary.

Op. 1080E EVAPORATION CONTROL SYSTEM

6

BOOT HOUSING SEAL CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the boot housing seal is correct

Move on to Step 7

Boot housing seal faulty

Check that the boot housing seal is correct

7

UNDERBODY CHECKS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the underbody plugs are present, in the area of the boot

End of diagnosis

No underbody plugs

Fit the missing plugs
