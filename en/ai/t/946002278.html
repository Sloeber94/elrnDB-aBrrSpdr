Removing

Refitting

1. Working under the right side of the dashboard, disconnect the electrical connections (1a, 1b).

Description

Component

FAN SPEED VARIATOR

See M072 FAN SPEED VARIATOR

2. Undo the bolts.

3. Remove the electronic variable valve timing.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the electronic variable valve timing is not damaged.

- Fit the electronic variable valve timing.

- Tighten the bolts.

- Connect the electrical connections.
