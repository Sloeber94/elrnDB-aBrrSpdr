Applies to: all engines; all production periods

After rain or washing, water penetration in the rear part of the passenger compartment is noticed.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK FOR PENETRATION THROUGH THE REARSCREEN

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check for sealant along the entire perimeter of the rearscreen, carrying out the

See Test 7035OA CHECK FOR ABSENCE OF FIXED WINDOW INFILTRATIONS (WINDSCREEN AND REAR SCREEN)

Move on to Step 2

Sealant missing

Restore the sealant

Op. 7035B04 REAR WINDSCREEN - ELIMINATE LEAKS

2

REARSCREEN CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the rearscreen. In particular, visually check:

- the condition of the glass

- the flatness of the contact surface

Move on to Step 3

Faults in rearscreen

Replace the rearscreen

Op. 7035B14 REAR WINDOW GLASS - R.R INCLUDES CLEANING HOUSING

3

TAILGATE SEAL CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the condition of the tailgate perimeter seal (no creases or wrinkles)

Move on to Step 4

Perimeter seal not correctly fitted.

Refit correctly. If necesssary, replace the seal

Op. 7015A30 TAILGATE COMPARTMENT SEAL - R+R

Perimeter seal faulty

Replace the seal

Op. 7015A30 TAILGATE COMPARTMENT SEAL - R+R

4

TAILGATE ALIGNMENT CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the fitting of the tailgate

In particular, make sure the alignment of the tailgate is correct checking that the seal is in contact along the entire perimeter

End of diagnosis

Tailgate fitting faulty

Restore the alignment of the tailgate

Op. 7015A14 TAILGATE - ADJUST CENTRING AND PROFILE - INCLUDES HINGE AND COMPARTMENT SEAL ADAPTATION
