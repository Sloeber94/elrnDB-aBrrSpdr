Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS; all production periods

- When the engine is cold, check that the level of coolant in the reservoir reaches the MAX reference on the reservoir.

- If necessary, top up with the specified fluid to the MAX reference.

Do not remove the tank cap when the engine is warm as there is a risk of scalding.
