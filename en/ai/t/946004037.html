Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods; only with equipment: Spider

While driving with the hood open (in Spider models) , noise can be heard coming from the hood area.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods; only with equipment: Spider

Test results

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check whether the noise only occurs when the hood is open.

Move on to Step 1

The noise is heard when the hood is closed.

Proceed as instructed

See table S090 HOOD NOISY - WITH HOOD CLOSED

1

WIND STOPPER FASTENING CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the wind stopper is correctly fastened

Move on to Step 2

Wind stopper not correctly fastened

Restore the correct fastening of the wind stopper.

2

CENTRE GUIDE BUFFER CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the presence / condition of the centre guide buffer on the hood and on the body

Move on to Step 3

Centre guide buffer not present / not intact

Refit the centre guide buffer correctly

3

HOOD COVER FRAME BUFFERS CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the two rubber buffers fitted at the bottom of the hood cover frame for containing judders whilst driving are present and are not faulty.

Move on to Step 4

Two rubber buffers have / do not have faults.

Refit the two buffers correctly

4

CORRECT ALIGNMENT OF HOOD COVER CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the hood cover is correctly aligned in the closed position.

End of diagnosis

Faults in the hood cover adjustment

Restore the correct hood cover adjustment

Op. 7030H05 HOOD - ADJUSTMENT
