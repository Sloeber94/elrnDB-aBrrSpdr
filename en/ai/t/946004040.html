After rain or washing, water penetration through the hood is noticed.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods; only with equipment: Spider

Test results

1

HOOD FRONT SEAL CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

In particular, check:

- the condition of the windscreen surround seals;

- for dirt, leaves etc.

- for tears/kinks on the hood front seal.

Move on to Step 2

Dirt in the windscreen surround seals.

Clean the windscreen surround seals.

Tears in the hood front seal

Restore the correct fitting of the hood front seal

Op. 7030H35 HOOD FRONT SEAL - R.R.

2

DOOR HOUSING SEAL CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the door housing seals. In particular, check:

- for kinks in the seals;

- the condition of the seal and the correct matching between the three seals.

Move on to Step 3

Seal fitting faulty

Refit the seals correctly

Op. 7005A34 FRONT SIDE DOOR COMPARTMENT SEAL - R.R

3

HOOD REAR SEAL CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the condition of the hood rear seal and check for tears/kinks, then make sure that it is correctly matched with the fixed seal on the hood cloth.

Move on to Step 4

Tears in the hood rear seal.

Restore the correct fitting of the hood rear seal

Op. 7030H39 HOOD REAR SEAL - R.R.

4

DOOR WINDOWS ADJUSTMENT CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the windows are correctly adjusted, in particular check:

- that the hood side seal adheres correctly to the glass and slides properly when the windows are opened or closed.

Move on to Step 5

Faults in the window adjustment

Restore the correct window adjustment

Op. 7005D11 LOWERING WINDOW (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT FOR FRONT SIDE DOOR - CHECK SLIDING - INCLUDES POSITIONING GUIDES, POSITIONING AND FASTENING WINDOW SUPPORT TO CONTROL

5

HOOD COVER COMPARTMENT OUTER TRIM CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the hood cover compartment outer trim seal is correct.

End of diagnosis

No sealant between the outer trim and the hood cover compartment.

Restore the correct sealant for the trim.
