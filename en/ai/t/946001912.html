Removing

Refitting

- Make sure that the ignition key is in the OFF position, then disconnect the battery terminal (-).

1. Disconnect the electrical connection from the engine coolant temperature sensor.

2. Undo the engine coolant temperature sensor and remove it.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the engine coolant temperature sensor in its housing and tighten it to the recommended torque.

Replace the seal.
