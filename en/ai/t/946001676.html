Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the doors are opened using the remote control, the fuel filler flap is not released.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

FUSE CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the condition of the protective fuse

See E3045 BOOT AND FUEL FLAP OPENING

Move on to Step 2

Fuse blown

Replace the fuse (and find out what could have caused it to blow).

2

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Connect the diagnostic instrument and check for errors in the body computer node.

Move on to Step 3

Errors present

Continue as instructed by the diagnostic equipment

3

ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the electrical connection of the fuel flap opening pawl.

See E3045 BOOT AND FUEL FLAP OPENING

Move on to Step 4

Electrical connector on boot lock incorrectly connected

Connect the electrical connector to the boot lock correctly

See E3045 BOOT AND FUEL FLAP OPENING

4

ELECTRICAL SIGNAL CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Disconnect the fuel flap opening pawl electrical connector and check the signal controlling door opening/closure from the remote control is present.

Move on to Step 5

Opening control signal missing

Move on to Step 7

5

RELAY CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the lock motor control relay is operating correctly.

Move on to Step 6

Relay defective

Replace the defective relay.

6

CHECK FUEL FLAP LOCK GEARED MOTOR

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the fuel flap lock is working properly.

End of diagnosis

Fuel filler flap lock motor defective

Replace the fuel flap lock motor

Op. 7025C40 FUEL FILLER FLAP CONTROL MOTOR - R+R AND CHECK LOCK SUPPLY LINE

7

ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the electrical connection between the Body Computer Node and the relay

See E3045 BOOT AND FUEL FLAP OPENING

Move on to Step 8

Connections broken or not insulated

Restore the continuity/insulation of the electrical connection

See E3045 BOOT AND FUEL FLAP OPENING

8

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that it is working properly

End of diagnosis

The fault persists.
