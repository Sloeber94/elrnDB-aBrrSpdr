Disconnect the sensor connection

See K025 BRAKE FLUID LEVEL SENSOR (SWITCH)

Connect the diagnostic equipment in Ohmmeter mode to the sensor terminals.

Check that the contact is open when the fluid level is low and closed when the level is correct.
