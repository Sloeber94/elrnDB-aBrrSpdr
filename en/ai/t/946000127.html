FUNCTIONAL DESCRIPTION

The electronic device inside the steering column switch unit H5 and the controls on the right lever receive a power supply at pin 11 of connector A from the ignition-controlled line (INT/A) of relay T12 protected by fuse F43, both housed in the junction unit under the dashboard B2.

Pin 7 of connector A and 9 of connector C of H5 are earthed.

The electronic device inside the steering column switch unit H5, which controls the rear wiper receive a power supply at pin 12 of connector C from the ignition-controlled line (INT/A) of relay T12 protected by fuse F52, both housed in the junction unit under the dashboard B2.

Steering wheel stalk unit H5 sends a command to rear wiper motor N16 from pin 1 of connector B while pin 2 receives an earth signal from the motor end stop cam, which stops operation.

Pin 3 of connector A of H5 receives the reverse engaged signal for automatic rear wiper operation.

The steering column switch unit H5 windscreen washer switch, once activated, sends two signals from pins 10 and 12 of connector A which operate the windscreen washer pump N22 which turns sending the washer fluid to the windscreen wiper.
