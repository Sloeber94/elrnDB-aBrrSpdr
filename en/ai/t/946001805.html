Removing

Refitting

Op. 7055B54 UNDER ENGINE PROTECTION/GUARD - R.R.

1. Undo the bolts fixing the power unit lower reaction rod to the engine oil sump.

2. Using tool (2a), undo the bolts fixing power unit lower reaction rod (2b) to the front suspension frame and remove it.
