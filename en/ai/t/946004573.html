Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS; all production periods

STEP

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

REMEDIAL ACTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Battery voltage check

Press the Start / Stop button and check that the battery voltage is greater than 11 V

Restore correct battery charge

2

Checking the hydraulic circuit pressure

- Connect Examiner and take a reading of the hydraulic circuit pressure. With the electric pump off, the pressure should be between 37.5 and 46 bar when the oil temperature is above 20°C

- Check gearbox oil level

Restore oil level and check there are no leaks in the system.

Renew the seal if there are leaks

3

Checking the Electric pump

Check the electric pump protective fuse is intact

Connect Examiner and in active diagnosis mode check operation of the electric pump.

Shift through the gears until the electric pump is activated and use a clamp ammeter to check that pump consumption is approximately 18 A, with battery voltage at least 11.5 V

Replace the 30 A fuse F8 after having verified the cause of malfunction

Replace the electric pump

Op. 2127C30 HYDRAULIC SPEED SELECTION SYSTEM PUMP - R.R.

Restore the continuity of the wiring between gearbox control unit and the electric pump

See E7051 ROBOTIZED GEARBOX

Restore the correct electric pump earth connection

See E7051 ROBOTIZED GEARBOX
