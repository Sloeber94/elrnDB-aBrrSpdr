When the alarm is activated, the direction indicators do not flash

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Test results

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

The flashing of the direction indicators may or may not be activated depending on the arrangement for the various countries.

Connect the diagnostic equipment to the diagnostic sockect and check for faults in the CAN.

Move on to Step 2

Presence of faults in the CAN

Restore the correct connections at the nodes involved.

2

ACTIVE DIAGNOSIS WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Using the diagnostic equipment, carry out the active diagnosis for the activation of the direction indicators.

End of diagnosis

Lights are not activated
