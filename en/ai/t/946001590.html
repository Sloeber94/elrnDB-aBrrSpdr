Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The MIL warning light does not come with the ignition on;

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

the engine failure warning light is switched on by means of an earth command directly from the "engine management node"; during normal operation the engine failure warning light should come on when the ignition is on and go out when the engine is started up.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Connect the diagnostic equipment to the diagnostic socket and check the operation of the engine failure warning light through active diagnosis at the engine management node

Move on to Step 2

1.The warning light does not come on

1.Renew the correct wiring

See E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL

2.The warning light comes on

2.Replace instrument panel

Op. 5560B10 CONTROL PANEL - R+R

2

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Replace the engine management control unit

Op. 1056F82 CONTROL UNIT (ONE) FOR INJECTION IGNITION SYSTEM - R.R.
