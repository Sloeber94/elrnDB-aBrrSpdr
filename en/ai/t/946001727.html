Applies to: all engines; all production periods

After rain or washing, water penetration in the area of one of the doors is noticed

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

DOOR RAIN GUTTER CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the door rain gutter. In particular, check:

- for dirt, leaves etc.

- that it is correctly fitted in the area of the corner (the seal should not cover the special air inlet opening)

Move on to Step 2

Dirt in the rain gutter

Clean the rain gutter

Rain gutter fitting faulty

Refit the rain gutter correctly

Op. 7055A12 RAIN GUTTER MOULDING ON ROOF - R.R.

2

DOOR HOUSING SEAL CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the door housing seal. In particular, check:

- for kinks in the seal

- the condition of the seal.

Move on to Step 3

Seal not correctly fitted

Refit correctly. If necesssary, replace the seal

Op. 7005A34 FRONT SIDE DOOR COMPARTMENT SEAL - R.R

Seal faulty

Replace the seal

Op. 7005A34 FRONT SIDE DOOR COMPARTMENT SEAL - R.R

3

DOOR ALIGNMENT CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the fitting of the door. In particular, check:

- correct alignment of the door-housing

- seal adheres correctly to the door

Move on to Step 4

Door fitting faulty

Restore the alignment of the door

Op. 7005A14 FRONT SIDE DOOR - ADJUST CENTERING AND MOULDING - INCLUDES ADAPTATION OF HINGES AND SEALS ON DOOR COMPARTMENT

4

MOVING GLASS CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the moving glass seal. In particular, make sure: - that the glass is properly closed - the glass channel is correctly positioned - the glass scraper is correctly fitted

Move on to Step 5

Channel fitting faulty

Refit the channel correctly

Op. 7005D29 FRONT CHANNEL/SEAL FOR FRONT SIDE DOOR LOWERING WINDOW GLASS (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

Glass fitting faulty

Refit the window opening mechanism and the moving glass correctly

Op. 7005D11 LOWERING WINDOW (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT FOR FRONT SIDE DOOR - CHECK SLIDING - INCLUDES POSITIONING GUIDES, POSITIONING AND FASTENING WINDOW SUPPORT TO CONTROL

Glass scraper fitting faulty

Refit the glass scraper correctly

Op. 7005D26 FRONT SIDE DOOR DESCENDING WINDOW GLASS SCRAPER (OUTER) (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT- R.R

5

DOOR DRAINAGE CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the drainage openings in the lower part of the door.

In particular, check for dirt, leaves, etc.

End of diagnosis

Dirt in the drains

Clean the drains. If necessary, refit the seal correctly

Op. 7005A50 FRONT SIDE DOOR WEATHERSTRIP - R.R.
