Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the lever on the right of the steering is pushed towards it, the rearscreen washer function is not activated properly, but the rearscreen wiper is working

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

First of all check the level of the detergent fluid in the reservoir and also make sure that it is not leaking

Move on to Step 1

Mixture level too low.

Restore the correct level.

Reservoir damaged.

Replace the reservoir

Op. 5050A10 WINDSCREEN WASHER FLUID RESERVOIR (IN ENGINE COMPARTMENT) - R+R

1

PIPE AND JET CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that there are no obstructions or incorrect connections for the rearscreen washer pipes. Check the condition of the jet

Move on to Step 2

Pipes blocked or damaged

Repair or replace the pipes

Jet obstructed or damaged

Repair or replace the jet

Op. 5050C10 REAR WINDOW WASHER JET - R+R CLEAN AND/OR POSITION

2

REARSCREEN WASHER ELECTRIC PUMP POWER SUPPLY

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the connection between the steering column switch unit and the rearscreen washer pump : wiring diagram

See E3021 REAR WINDOW WASH/WIPE

Move on to Step 3

Connection broken

Repair the connections: wiring diagram

See E3021 REAR WINDOW WASH/WIPE

3

REARSCREEN WASHER PUMP CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the operation of the rearscreen washer electric pump

It is the actual pump that carries out the screen washer function but operated in the reverse direction

Move on to Step 4

Pump faulty.

Replace the screen washer electric pump

Op. 5050B20 WINDSCREEN WASHER ELECTRICAL PUMP - R+R

4

STEERING COLUMN SWITCH UNIT CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the stalk unit is working properly: in particular the rearscreen wiper switch

See E3021 REAR WINDOW WASH/WIPE

End of diagnosis

Stalk unit not working properly.

Replace the steering column switch unit.
