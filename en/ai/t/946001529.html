The direction indicators do not come on when the hazard warning lights button is pressed

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment to the diagnostic socket and check the following at the Body Computer Node:

- the plausibiilty of the hazard warning lights on/off parameter

End of diagnosis

Parameter does not change status

Move on to Step 2

2

ELECTRICAL SYSTEM CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the continuity of the wiring between the hazard warning lights button and the Body Computer Node.

See E2020 DIRECTION INDICATORS / HAZARD WARNING LIGHTS

Move on to Step 3

Wiring broken

Restore the correct connections

See E2020 DIRECTION INDICATORS / HAZARD WARNING LIGHTS

3

ELECTRICAL SYSTEM CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check hazard warning lights button earth

See E2020 DIRECTION INDICATORS / HAZARD WARNING LIGHTS

Move on to Step 4

Earth interrupted

Restore the correct earth

See E2020 DIRECTION INDICATORS / HAZARD WARNING LIGHTS

4

CHECK ON OPERATION OF CONTROL BUTTON

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check operation of hazard warning light control switch

Move on to Step 5

Hazard warning light switch internal circuit broken

Replace the hazard warning light control switch

Op. 7040L37 GEAR LEVER TRIM - R.R

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
