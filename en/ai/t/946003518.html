The diagnostic equipment signals: Error P0778 (fuel pressure regulator (high pressure pump outlet or pipe).

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

This error is detected if there is a break in the circuit connected to Pins 4 and 34 of the control unit for at least 0.28 secs. (detail "open circuit"), if there is a short circuit to positive in the circuit connected to Pin 34 of the control unit for at least 0.28 secs (detail "short circuit to +Vbatt"), if there is a short circuit to earth in the circuit connected to Pin 34 of the control unit for at least 0.22 secs. (detail "short circuit to earth") or if the temperature of the electronic control module measured is not within the limits for at least 0.28 secs (detail "excessive temperature").

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

1

Check on operation of fuel pressure regulator on the rail

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED
