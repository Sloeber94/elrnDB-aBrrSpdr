Removing

Refitting

1. Disconnect the electrical connections (1a) and (1b) for the injection-ignition control unit (1c).

Description

Connector

1a

Engine management control unit

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

Description

Connector

1b

Engine management control unit

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

2. Loosen the nuts securing the mounting brackets and remove the injection-ignition control unit (1c).

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the injection-ignition control unit back in its housing and secure it to the mounting bracket using the nuts.

- Connect the electrical connections for the injection-ignition control unit.
