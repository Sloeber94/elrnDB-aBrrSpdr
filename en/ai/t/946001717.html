Operation

First of all check that the outside aerial is correctly fastened.

Check that the wind noise is actually coming from the windscreen.

Move on to Step 1.

FRONT WIND NOISE LOCATION CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

First of all check that the wind noise is coming from the windscreen.

Carry out the test for the location of the wind noise from the windscreen

See Test 7035AA WINDSCREEN NOISE LOCALISATION

End of diagnosis
