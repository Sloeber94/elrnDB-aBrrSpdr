Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

ATTENTION

WARNINGS

This vehicle is equipped with a starting system known as Smart-Key where there is no longer a traditional ignition key having been replaced with an electronic key "T.E.G." (Transmitter for Entry and go) combined with an electronic key reader and TEG-Reader radio frequency receiver.

When the battery is disconnected, the Steering Lock Node remains in the same condition (released or locked) as previously. Whenever it is necessary to turn the steering wheel with the battery disconnected during workshop operations, leave the TEG inserted when disconnecting the battery: the steering will thereby remain unlocked and free to turn.

INITIALISIATION PROCEDURE FOR DOOR NODES

Following a power supply failure to the control units (for example, battery disconnection) the system must be initialized once again, following the instructions below and working with the ignition key ON (TEG inserted) and the doors and windows closed.

- completely open the driver's door window, keeping the operating button pressed for at least 3 seconds after the (lower) end of travel position

- completely close the driver's door window, keeping the operating button pressed for at least 3 seconds after the (upper) end of travel position

- carry out the same operations for the passenger window

- make sure that the initialization is correct by checking that the windows work properly in automatic mode.
