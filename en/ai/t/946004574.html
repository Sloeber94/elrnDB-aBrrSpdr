Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS; all production periods

STEP

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

REMEDIAL ACTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Checking gearbox oil electric pump operation

Press the Start/Stop button

Connect the Examiner Plus or Smart diagnostic equipment.

Using the Examiner Plus or Smart, check that the battery voltage is more than 11.5 V.

Shift through the gears until the electric pump is activated.

Move on to Step 2

2

Checking the electrical system

Check the continuity/insulation of the wiring between the oil pump and the gearbox control unit

Check the 30 A fuse F8 on the battery fuses control unit is intact

Check the continuity/insulation of the wiring between the engine compartment junction unit and the gearbox control unit

Replace the 30 A fuse F8 after having verified the cause of malfunction.

See E7051 ROBOTIZED GEARBOX

Restore the continuity of the wiring between gearbox control unit and the electric pump

Restore the correct electric pump earth connection

See E7051 ROBOTIZED GEARBOX

3

Check using Examiner

Connect Examiner and in active diagnosis mode check operation of the electric pump.

Use a clamp ammeter to check that pump consumption is approximately 18 A, with battery voltage at least 11.5 V

Replace the electric pump

Op. 2127C30 HYDRAULIC SPEED SELECTION SYSTEM PUMP - R.R.

If the previous checks have not revealed any faults

Op. 2127E10 HYDRAULIC SPEED SELECTION SYSTEM CONTROL UNIT - R.R.

Replace the robotized gearbox control unit
