S062 FRONT DOORS CREAKING

Path

fault diagnosis table (symptom -> numbered test steps)
Applies to

Interactive page

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the vehicle moving, particularly over rough ground or during acceleration or braking, noise can be heard from the one of the doors.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Test results

1

CHECK DASHBOARD/DOOR PANEL COUPLING

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the door panel and dashboard are correctly coupled

Move on to Step 2

Faulty dashboard fastening

Restore the correct fastening of the dashboard

Op. 7040A10 DASHBOARD TRIM - R+R

Restore the felt pads if necessary

2

CHECK DOOR FASTENINGS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the fastening:

- of the door opening limiter

- of the door hinges (upper and lower)

- of the door lock

Move on to Step 3

Faults in limiter fastening

Lubricate the limiter, if necessary. Renew the correct fastening

Op. 7005A28 FRONT SIDE DOOR LIMITER - R.R.

Faults in the hinge fastening

Lubricate the hinge pins, if necessary. Renew the correct fastening

Op. 7005A14 FRONT SIDE DOOR - ADJUST CENTERING AND MOULDING - INCLUDES ADAPTATION OF HINGES AND SEALS ON DOOR COMPARTMENT

Faults in lock fastening

Renew the correct fastening

Op. 7005N21 FRONT SIDE DOOR LOCK MOTOR/LOCK ASSEMBLEY FOR ONE DOOR, LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

Faults in lock striker fastening

Renew the correct fastening

Op. 7005B14 FRONT SIDE DOOR LOCK STRIKER - R.R

3

CHECK DOOR PANEL

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Remove the door panel

Op. 7005E10 FRONT SIDE DOOR INTERIOR PANEL - R.R.

and check there are no foreign bodies inside the door

Move on to Step 4

Foreign bodies present

Remove the foreign bodies

4

CHECK DOOR INNER FASTENINGS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the fastening:

- of the window mechanism

- of the moving window guides

- of the moving window fastening element

- of the door opening/closure linkage

End of diagnosis

Faults in window mechanism fastening

Renew the correct fastening

Op. 7005M25 FRONT SIDE DOOR ELECTRICALLY OPERATED WINDOW MECHANISM - CHECK POSITION AND FASTENING

Faults in guide fastening

Renew the correct fastening

Op. 7005D29 FRONT CHANNEL/SEAL FOR FRONT SIDE DOOR LOWERING WINDOW GLASS (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

Faults in fastening element

Renew the correct fastening

Op. 7005D11 LOWERING WINDOW (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT FOR FRONT SIDE DOOR - CHECK SLIDING - INCLUDES POSITIONING GUIDES, POSITIONING AND FASTENING WINDOW SUPPORT TO CONTROL

Faults in linkage fastening

Restore the linkage retaining clips

Op. 7005C FRONT DOOR LOCK CONTROLS
