Refitting

Removing

1. Fit a suitable pin (diameter 1.5 mm) to secure the water pump/balance shaft drive chain hydraulic tensioner.

Press the tensioner piston and turn to the right to fit the tool.

2. Refit water pump/balancer shaft drive chain hydraulic tensioner (2a) in its housing and secure by tightening bolts (2b) to the specified torque.
