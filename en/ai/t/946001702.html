Test results

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Adjust the backrest by pressing with your hands to identify the sources of the noise coming from the backrest

Move on to Step 1

Components with interference

Move the interfering components apart and, if the welding is insufficient, renew it

1

CHECK ON MATCHING OF UPPER AND LOWER CROSSMEMBERS WITH BACKREST SIDES

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the areas where the upper and lower crossmembers are matched with the backrest sides

Move on to Step 2

Breaks in the matching areas

Replace the backrest assembly

2

CHECK ON SPRING PLATE FASTENING

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the spring plate rivets are correctly fastened to the retaining borders.

Also check for any broken springs

Move on to Step 3

1. Rivets detached/loose

2. Springs broken

1. Renew the spring fastening rivets and/or reattach the retaining border

2. Replacement of backrest springs assembly

3

CREAKING CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check on creaking caused by whiplash mechanism on backrest

Move on to Step 4

1. Joint springs creaking

2. Contacts with backrest internal components (frame, etc.)

1. Lubricate springs/joints with molybdenum disulphide grease

2. Space out the parts in contact

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

The fault persists

Replace the seat frame
