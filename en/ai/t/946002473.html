Op. 7005E11 LH OR RH FRONT DOOR INNER PANEL - R+R WITH FOULING PARTS REMOVED

1. Working on the inner door panel, gently slide the inner glass scraper (1a) in order to access the warning light (1b).

2. Undo the bolt.

3. Extract the warning light.

4. If necessary, remove the outer trim.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the warning light is not damaged.

- If removed, refit the outer trim.

- Insert the warning light in position.

- Tighten the bolt.

- Slide the inner glass scraper into position.
