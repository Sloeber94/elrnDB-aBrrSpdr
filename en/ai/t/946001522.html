Test results

1

CHECK ON CONNECTOR AT HEADLAMP

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the 14-way connector on the master/slave lamp is correctly fitted

Move on to Step 2

Connector not correctly fitted

Refit correctly

See E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS

2

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that there are no errors by connecting to the Master (left side) or Slave (right side) headlamp using the diagnostic equipment

Move on to Step 3

Error B1010 detected - xenon lamp fails to come on

Move on to Step 3

Error B1012 detected - xenon lamp voltage out of the range

Move on to Step 3

Error B1000 detected - control unit failure

Replace ballast control unit

Op. 5540B40 CONTROL UNIT IN LEFT GAS DISCHARGE LIGHT CLUSTER - R.R.

and/or

Op. 5540B41 CONTROL UNIT IN RIGHT GAS DISCHARGE LIGHT CLUSTER - R.R.

3

DIPPED HEADLAMPS POWER SUPPLY CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the headlamps are receiving the correct power supply, by carrying out the

See Test 5540BA DIPPED BEAM CIRCUIT CHECK

Move on to Step 4

4

HEADLAMP INTERNAL WIRING CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the screened connector for the headlamp bulb is correctly fitted in the ballast control unit.

Check that the screened connector for the bulb is correctly fitted in place.

Check the continuity of the wiring between the 14-way connector for the headlamp and the connector connected to the ballast control unit, by carrying out the

See Test 5540BK CHECK THE CONTINUITY OF THE LOW BEAM WIRING INSIDE THE HEADLAMP

Move on to Step 5

Screened connector not correctly fitted in the control unit

Fit the connector correctly in the control unit

See E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS

Bulb connector not correctly fitted

Fit the bulb connector correctly

See E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS

Connection broken

Move on to Step 6

5

BULB OPERATION CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the fault disappears by replacing the bulb

Op. 5540B45 GAS DISCHARGE DIPPED/MAIN BEAM HEADLAMP BULB - R.R.

End of diagnosis

The fault persists

Refit the original bulb and move on to Step 6

6

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION OF HEADLAMP

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Replace the left headlamp

Op. 5540B81 LEFT GAS DISCHARGE LIGHT CLUSTER - R.R.

or right headlamp

Op. 5540B82 RIGHT GAS DISCHARGE LIGHT CLUSTER - R.R.

(the failed unit) and check it is working properly

End of diagnosis

The fault persists

Refit the headlamp replaced previously and move on to step 7

7

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Replace the faulty headlamp ballast control unit

Op. 5540B40 CONTROL UNIT IN LEFT GAS DISCHARGE LIGHT CLUSTER - R.R.

and/or

Op. 5540B41 CONTROL UNIT IN RIGHT GAS DISCHARGE LIGHT CLUSTER - R.R.

End of diagnosis

The fault persists

Contact technical service for further information on the problem
