Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods

FUNCTIONAL DESCRIPTION

Versions with automatic transmission:

when reverse gear is engaged, automatic transmission control unit M55 sends the reverse gear engaged signal from pin 13 of connector A to relay T20 in engine compartment control unit B1, which receives an ignition-operated (INT) power supply from the line for fuse F35 of under-dashboard control unit B2 and sends a power supply to the reversing light in the right rear light cluster - moving section - F36, directly via control unit B2, from pin 7 of connector D.

Versions with robotized gearbox:

when reverse gear is engaged, the robotized gearbox control unit M54 sends a power supply directly to the reversing light in the right rear light cluster (moving section) F36, directly via the control unit B2 from pin 7 of connector D.

The activation signal for the reversing light is also sent to the Body Computer M1 - pin 3 of connector F - and from there to the steering wheel interface H5, for the automatic engagement of the rearscreen wiper.

See E3021 REAR WINDOW WASH/WIPE
