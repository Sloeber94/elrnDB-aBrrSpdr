1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the crankshaft front oil seal cover (1b).

To remove the crankshaft front oil seal cover, move forward first to release the two locating bushes.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS; all production periods

The crankshaft front oil seal cover gasket does not need replacing whenever the cover is removed but only if it is damaged.

1. Remove the crankshaft front oil seal cover (1a) destructively by cutting through points (1b) shown in the figure.

The part of the gasket (1c) that remains attached to the rigid engine mount does not affect the seal. Remove the same section from the new gasket to allow installation.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS; all production periods

- Working at the bench, remove the oil seal from the timing chain cover.

Take care not to damage the sealing surfaces during the "destructive" removal of the oil seal.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS; all production periods
