Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods

- Check the tyre tread is not worn.

Some types of tyre are fitted with wear gauges; they should be replaced as soon as these gauges are visible on the tread.

Do not swap tyres across.

- Check the tyre inflation pressure is correct. Also check the spare wheel.

The tyre pressures must be checked when they are cold.

- Check that the lighting system is operating correctly: headlamps, direction indicators, hazard warning lights, luggage compartment light, passenger compartment light, oddments compartment light and instrument panel warning lights.

- Check the windscreen wiper system is operating correctly and adjust the jets if necessary.

- Check the front and rear wiper blades are correctly positioned and not worn.

- Check condition and wear of front disc brake pads and operation of pad wear indicator

- Carry out a visual check of: exterior bodywork, underbody protection, rigid and flexible pipes (exhaust - fuel system - braking system) rubber elements (boots - hoses - bushes - etc.)

- Check cleanliness of locks, bonnet and boot lids, cleanliness and lubrication of linkage

- For Spider versions only:

check that the hood opens/closes correctly;

visually inspect the hood seals;

check that the windows adhere to the hood seal and adjust if necessary.

- Check and, if necessary, top up the engine fluid levels: brake/clutch, power assisted steering, screen washer, battery, engine cooling, etc.

- Connect the Examiner diagnostic equipment to the vehicle diagnostic socket and check the engine operation.

At the end of the service, connect the Examiner diagnostic equipment to the on board diagnostic socket to zero the mileage counter for the days to the starting value and write the details of the service that has been carried out (date, service no., total mileage) in the Trip Computer memory which can only be read using the diagnostic equipment.

Check that the figure has been set in the instrument panel in km or miles depending on the unit of measurement in that country.
