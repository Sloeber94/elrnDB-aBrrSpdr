0010T71 POWER STEERING FLUID CHECK LEVEL AND TOP UP IF NECESSARY

Path

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS; all production periods

Perform this operation with the vehicle level and the engine off and cold.

1. Check that the power steering fluid level corresponds to the MAX notch visible on the reservoir or the upper notch (maximum level) on the dipstick attached to the reservoir cap.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS; all production periods

- If the reservoir fluid level is lower than specified, top up as described below.

- Start the engine and wait for the fluid level in the tank to stabilise.

- With the engine running, fully turn the steering wheel from right to left several times.

- Top up with the recommended fluid until the fluid level reaches the MAX mark and then refit the plug.

Do not allow the power steering fluid to come into contact with hot parts of the engine. it is flammable.
