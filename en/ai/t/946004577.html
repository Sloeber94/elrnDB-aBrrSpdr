Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS; all production periods

STEP

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

REMEDIAL ACTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Check on button signal

Using the Examiner in voltmeter mode connect between pin 2 and earth for the SPORT button connector

See E7051 ROBOTIZED GEARBOX

Press the button and check that the voltage is around 150 mv

Release the button and check that the voltage is around 5 Volt

Restore the correct continuity for the wiring between the gearbox control unit and the button

2

Check earth connection

Disconnect the connector for the button and check that the resistance between pin 1 and earth is less than 1 ohm

See E7051 ROBOTIZED GEARBOX

Restore the earth connection

Replace the robotized gearbox control unit

Op. 2127E10 HYDRAULIC SPEED SELECTION SYSTEM CONTROL UNIT - R.R.

3

Check on SPORT LED power supply

Insert the TEG in the reader

Connect the voltmeter between pin 4 of the Sport button connector and earth to check that the battery supply voltage is correct at 12 Volt

See E7051 ROBOTIZED GEARBOX

Replace the 7.5 A fuse F35 in the junction unit under the dashboard after having verified the cause of malfunction.

Restore the wiring between the dashboard control unit and the Sport button

4

Check on operation of Normal/Sport button

Press the Normal/Sport button and check continuity of contacts between pins 1 and 2

See E7051 ROBOTIZED GEARBOX

Replace the Sport button

Op. 2127E30 SOFTWARE FUNCTION SELECTION KEY (ONE) FOR ELECTROHYDRAULIC SELECTION GEARBOX - R.R.
