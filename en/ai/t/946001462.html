Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The siren is connected to the Body Computer Node via the A-Bus dialogue line.The siren is operated and controlled by the Body Computer Node by means of a series of coded messages; in the case of a failure the Body Computer node detects and memorizes the causes of the malfunction.The siren is equipped with a battery whose charge is guaranteed in the presence of a +15 supply from the ignition with the alarm system switched off and is managed by the actual siren; the activation/end of charge command is given by the Body Computer

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
