Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods

DESCRIPTION

The vehicle is equipped with a white reversing light located in the passenger side rear light cluster.

It is activated each time reverse gear is engaged: this information arrives from the automatic transmission, or the robotized gearbox, control unit.

The circuit for the reversing lights is supplied with the TEG inserted (INT) and is protected by a special fuse in the under-dashboard control unit.

The signal that reverse gear has been engaged is managed by the Body Computer which forwards this information to the steering wheel interface for the automatic operation of the rearscreen wiper and, via the CAN, to the other nodes, for example to the parking sensors control unit.
