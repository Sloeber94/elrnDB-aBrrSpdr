SPRING/SHOCK ABSORBER ASSEMBLY CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that:

- all three threads for the bolts securing the shock absorber spring assembly to the bodyshell are clearly visible and the same height;

on the left side of the vehicle in the direction of travel, the presence of a reinforcement at the height of the thread coming out of the front nut.

- any abrasions to the body paintwork around the fastenings (sliding fastening);

- the presence and/or correct positioning of the lower rubber shim by carrying out the

See Test 4410AB CHECKING RUBBER BLOCK AND BEARING ON FRONT SHOCK ABSORBER

CHECK ON UPPER TRACK CONTROL ARM PIN AND BUSHES

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the ball pin boot does not show signs of notches or traces of grease.

Undo the pin

Op. 4410B68 UPPER TRACK CONTROL ARM (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT FOR FRONT SUSPENSION - R.R. CHECK WHEEL GEOMETRY SEPARATELY

move the threaded pin manually and check for any hardening/sticking. Working by hand on the track control arm, gradually increase the force until the arm does not move, checking for any creaking of the bushes. Repeat the operation described above more often and pulling the arm to highlight any noise due to possible clearance; if there is any noise, visually check that the yield point type bolts tightening the track control arm bushes have not become loose.

ANTI-ROLL BAR CHECKS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that there are no faults with the anti-roll bar

See Test 4410CA CHECKS ON STABILIZER BAR

More specifically:

  • Anti-roll bar correctly positioned in relation to the centre-line of the vehicle
  • condition (crumbling) of centre flexible mountings in the contact area with the bar;
  • Front fastenings tightened to torque between bar and chassis
  • Fastenings tightened to torque between bar and shock absorber connecting rod.

Also check, after having disconnected the upper track rod end for the connecting rod at the shock absorber fork, that the anti-roll bar does not creak at the centre flexible mountings when moved manually with a symmetrical/asymmetrical load applied and that the loose connecting rod does not move unevenly or stick.Check the condition of the boots for the connecting rod joints and check for grease.

CHECK ON LOWER TRACK CONTROL ARM PIN AND BUSHES

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Visually check on the lower track control arm front bush that the fixing bolts have not loosened and check for any damage to the rubber mountings or plastic components.

Check that there are no glycol leaks from the lower track control arm rear bush.

If the creaking persists, remove the arm pin from the steering knuckle

Op. 4410B40 LOWER TRACK CONTROL ARM (WITH BALLJOINT) FOR LEFT OR RIGHT FRONT SUSPENSION (ONE) - R+R - CHECK WHEEL GEOMETRY SEPARATELY

checking that there is no sticking/hardening of the head through manual movements and also checking that the pin boot is intact.

Also check for rotation between the lower track control arm and the rear hydraulic bush due to the presence of rubber on the arm and damage to the rubber of the actual bush.
