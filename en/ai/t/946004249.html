Removing

Refitting

1. Working in the centre of the dashboard, place the air vents in the closed air flow position.

2. Unscrew the bolts.

3. Move vent assembly (3a) slightly by releasing flexible retainers (3b).

4. Detach the electrical connection.

Description

Component

CENTRAL FRONT SPEAKER

See P032 CENTRE FRONT SPEAKER

5. Unscrew the bolts.

6. Remove the speaker.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the front speaker is not damaged.

- Position the speaker.

- Screw in the bolts.

- Attach the electrical connection.

- Position the vent assembly and attach the retainers.

- Screw in the bolts.

- Check that the speaker is working.
