H011 STEERING HARD / (IN ONE DIRECTION ONLY)

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the steering is operated normally there is a hardening of the steering action to the right or the left

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Carry out a test steering fully in various conditions to check the actual onset of the hardening in one direction only.

Visually inspect the power assisted steering fluid level in the reservoir.

Move on to Step 1

1. Hardening in both directions

1. Proceed as instructed in the

See table H010 STEERING HARD / (STEERING IN BOTH DIRECTIONS)

2. Power assisted steering fluid level too low.

2. Top up to the correct level, if there are leaks, continue as instructed in the

See table X030 POWER ASSISTED STEERING SYSTEM FLUID LEAKS

1

STEERING RODS AND ARMS CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the steering rods and arms

Move on to Step 2

1. Rods damaged or worn.

1. Replace the rods

Op. 4110B10 STEERING LINKS - R+R ON VEHICLE - CHECK TOE-IN/TOE-OUT SEPARATELY

2. Arms damaged or worn.

2. Replace the steering box

Op. 4130A15 POWER STEERING BOX R R WITH FRONT SUSPENSION FRAME REMOVED

2

RACK AND PINION CLEARANCE CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the rack and pinion clearance with the steering column detached and the wheels straight.

Move on to Step 3

Rack and pinion clearance too large.

Replace the steering box

Op. 4130A15 POWER STEERING BOX R R WITH FRONT SUSPENSION FRAME REMOVED

3

CHECK ON CORRECT TENSION OF DRIVE BELT AND CORRECT OPERATION OF POWER ASSISTED STEERING PUMP

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the tension of the power assisted steering pump drive belt is correct and that it is not worn.

Also check that the power assisted steering pump is working properly.

Move on to Step 4

1. Belt not correctly tensioned.

1. Restore correct belt tension

Op. 1092C26 BELT FROM AIR CONDITIONER COMPRESSOR DRIVING POWER STEERING PUMP - R + R

2. Belt worn.

2. Replace the belt

Op. 1092C26 BELT FROM AIR CONDITIONER COMPRESSOR DRIVING POWER STEERING PUMP - R + R

3. Power assisted steering pump malfunction.

3. Replace the power assisted steering pump

Op. 4130B20 POWER STEERING PUMP - R.R.

4

STEERING WHEEL RETURN CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Refit all the components removed.

Test the vehicle and check that the steering wheel returns correctly.

End of diagnosis

Poor steering wheel return.

Restore the correct front wheel geometry

Op. 4450A10 FRONT WHEEL GEOMETRY - CHECK USING OPTICAL EQUIPMENT - INCLUDES ADJUSTMENT IF NECESSARY
