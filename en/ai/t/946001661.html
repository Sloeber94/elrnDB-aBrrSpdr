seat lifting lever loose, detached, loss of position, seat lifting "hard" during operation / unsoldered.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Test results

1

CHECK ON OPERATION OF RATCHET

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the condition of the fastening of the lever to the height adjustment device (ratchet)

Move on to Step 2

Lever and/or ratchet retaining elements broken

Replace the lever and/or fastening element (fixing bolt)

2

CHECK ON CONDITION OF RATCHET MOUNTING SYSTEM

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the condition of the seat lifting ratchet attachment system

Move on to Step 3

Ratchet loose (fastening elements to seat frame)

Retighten the ratchet to the frame correctly

3

CHECK ON SEAT LIFTING LEVER CONTACTS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check with the user that there are no "thigh/lever" contact when getting in/out of the vehicle

Move on to Step 4

Presence of contact

Notify the customer that this is not a defect of the seat lifting

4

CHECK ON HEIGHT ADJUSTMENT DEVICE

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the condition of the height adjustment device (ratchet) checking for the presence of looseness/clearance

Move on to Step 5

Presence of looseness/clearance

Replace the seat lifting ratchet

Op. 7045A56 DRIVER'S SEAT HEIGHT ADJUSTMENT DEVICE CONTROL LEVER - R.R

5

CHECK ON GEAR SYSTEM MATCHING

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the gear system is correctly matched

Move on to Step 6

Matching not correct, presence of clearance/interference

Remove the ratchet (removing the cauterization) and refit correctly

6

CHECK ON CONNECTING ROD HINGE PIN MATCHING

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the connecting rod hinge pins are correctly matched (clearance/seizing)

Move on to Step 7

Matching not correct

Replace the seat lifting assembly

Op. 7045A36 DRIVER'S SEAT MANUAL HEIGHT ADJUSTMENT DEVICE - R.R

7

CHECK ON OPERATION OF TORSION BAR/BUSH

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the operation/condition of the torsion bar/bush

End of diagnosis

Torsion bar detached/loose/broken

Replace the seat lifting assembly

Op. 7045A36 DRIVER'S SEAT MANUAL HEIGHT ADJUSTMENT DEVICE - R.R
