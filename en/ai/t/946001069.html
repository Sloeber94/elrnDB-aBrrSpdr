Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Do not check the air conditioning system in the immediate vicinity of the walls (distance of approximately 2 m) in order to ensure sufficient circulation of the air by the condenser.

STEP

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS OK

1

Connect the air conditioning system repressurizing station.

Read the value on the high pressure gauge and compare it with the A/C pressure parameter (air conditioning), checking that the two values more or less coincide

Carry out the

TP5040BD

2

Check that the condenser is not blocked with dirt

Clean the condenser fins or repair the radiator

3

Carry out the

TP5040AB
