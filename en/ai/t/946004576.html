STEP

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

REMEDIAL ACTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Check on UP button

Disconnect the connector for the wiring coming from the buttons from the clock spring

Connect the ohmmeter between pins 3 and 4

Press the UP button and

Check that the resistance value is 330 Ohm ± 10%

Press the DOWN button and

Check that the resistance value is 1015 Ohm ± 10%

Replace the UP button

Op. 2127A30

Replace the DOWN button

Op. 2127A30

Up/Down buttons connector
