7205E46 STRIPPING HOOD HOUSING COVER

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods

The operation should be carried out with the cover removed.

Op. 7210E47 HOOD HOUSING COMPARTMENT SIDE COVER - R.R.

Op. 7025D11 HOOD HOUSING COMPARTMENT COVER OUTER LINING - R.R.

Op. 7025D26 HOOD HOUSING COMPARTMENT COVER LOCK STRIKER (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

1. Undo the bolts securing the rod.

2. Remove the rod.

3. Working on both sides of the cover, undo the bolts (3a) and remove the half hinges (3b).

4. Working on both sides of the cover, undo the pins with a spherical mounting.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods

- Check that the hood housing cover is not damaged.
