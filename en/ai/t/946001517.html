Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The parking lights do not work under any circumstances;

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The switching on signal for the parking lights (side lights and the no. plate lights) is supplied directly by the Body Computer Node.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The driver turns on the parking lights by means of the PARK button;

operate the stalk to select:

1. Side lights only on - left or right

2. Side lights on - left and right

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

PARKING LIGHTS CONTROL CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the operation of the parking light button

See E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS

Move on to Step 2

Button will not operate

Replace the control panel

Op. 7040A56 SWITCH UNIT IN DASHBOARD - R.R.

2

ELECTRICAL SYSTEM CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the continuity and the insulation of the wiring between the PARK button and the Body Computer

See E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS

End of diagnosis

Connection between the PARK button and Body Computer Node short circuit or open circuit

Restore the correct electrical connection

See E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS

Body Computer defective

Replace Body Computer

Op. 5505A32 BODY COMPUTER - R.R
