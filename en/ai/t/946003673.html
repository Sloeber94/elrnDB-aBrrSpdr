Removing

Refitting

Op. 7030H20 HOOD FRONT PANEL - R.R.

1. Undo the bolts securing the lock.

2. Remove the retainer from the joint.

3. Remove the front lock (3a) releasing it from the joint (3b).

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the front lock is not damaged.

- Position the lock and connect it to the joint.

- Insert the joint retainer.

- Fit the lock retaining bolts without tightening them.

Op. 7030H20 HOOD FRONT PANEL - R.R.

- Adjust the hood.
