Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods

- There is a seal fitted on the top part of the connector, in contact with the control unit, and therefore for its removal it is necessary to refer to

Op. 2135B52 ELECTRONIC AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION ELECTRIC CIRCUIT -ECU MOUNT - R.R.

- The other seal is fitted on the lower part of the connector, inside the gearbox and therefore for its removal it is necessary to refer to

Op. 2135B58 ELECTRONIC AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION SYSTEM WIRING - R.R.
