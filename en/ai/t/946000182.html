Applies to: all engines; all production periods

FUNCTIONAL DESCRIPTION

The driver's seat control switch H66 and the passenger seat control switch H67 control the angle of the backrest by means of two relays J139 and J140.

The power supply for the two relays J139 and J140 arrives via fuses F56 and F60 located in the luggage compartment fuse box B45.

The coils for relays J139 and J140 receive a power supply from the ignition-operated line (INT) protected by fuse F49 of junction unit B2

When the switches H66 and H67 are operated in both directions they send a power supply and earth alternately to the two motors M132 and N135 which rotate in two opposite directions adjusting the angle of the seat.
