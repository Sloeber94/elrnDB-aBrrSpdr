Removing

Refitting

1. Remove the engine oil filter (1a) using a suitable tool (1b).

2. Remove the seal.

3. Extract the engine oil filter cartridge.

Collect the engine oil that comes out in a suitable container.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place a new engine oil filter cartridge in its housing.

- Place the engine oil filter cover complete with a new gasket in its housing and tighten it to the recommended torque using the tool.
