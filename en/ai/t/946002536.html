Removing

Refitting

1. Tilt the reflector (1a) downwards until it is in the end of travel position, then insert a plastic blade (1b) between the reflector (1a) and the shell (1c) and release the reflector retainers (1d) from the inner circular support.

2. Disconnect the electrical connections for the reflector defroster.

3. Keep the reflector.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the reflector is not damaged.

- Connect the electrical connections for the reflector defroster.

- Position the reflector and attach the retainers.

- Check that the reflector is working.
