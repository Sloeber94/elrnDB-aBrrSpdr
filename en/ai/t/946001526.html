Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The reversing lights are controlled directly by the switch on the transmission connected to the positive line.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

REVERSING SWITCH CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the reversing lights control switch is working correctly

Move on to Step 2

Switch with defective contact

Replace the reversing switch

Op. 5550D22 REVERSING LIGHT SWITCH - R+R

2

CHECK ON OPERATION OF FUSE AND RELAY

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the operation of relay and fuse in the engine compartment junction unit

Move on to Step 2

Relay defective

Replace the relay

Fuse blown

Replace the fuse

3

ELECTRICAL SYSTEM CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the continuity and the insulation of the wiring between the reversing switch and the reversing light

See E2022 REVERSING LIGHTS

Move on to Step 4

Switch wiring short circuited or open circuit

Restore the correct wiring

4

BULB OPERATION CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the reversing light bulb is working

Move on to Step 5

Bulb defective

Replace the reversing light bulb

Op. 5540A50 BULB FOR REAR LIGHT CLUSTER (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - REPLACE

5

BODY COMPUTER CONNECTION CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check for deformed terminals or connections not present on the Body Computer

Move on to Step 6

Missing connections/pins bent

Renew the correct connections

6

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

The fault persists

Replace Body Computer

Op. 5505A32 BODY COMPUTER - R.R
