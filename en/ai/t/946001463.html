Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the alarm is switched on using the remote control, the system is activated correctly but the volumetric sensors do not intervene.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The volumetric sensors operate at a hyperfrequency and are fitted in the two doors, one in each door and are connected to the Body Computer Node by means of a line known as A-BUS.The volumetric sensors are operated and controlled by the Body Computer via a series of encrypted messages and can be excluded by the customer using the special button. If there is a failure the Body Computer Node detects and memorizes the causes of the malfunction

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
