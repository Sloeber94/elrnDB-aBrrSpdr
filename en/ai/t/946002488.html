Refitting

1. Remove the water shield protection (1a), removing the adhesive bead (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the new protection is intact and not torn.

Always replace the water protection even if it is intact. The adhesive will not guarantee the necessary seal once it has been detached from the panel.

- Position the new water shield protection, ensuring that the adhesive bead sticks perfectly.

Check that the water shield protection adheres perfectly along the entire perimeter.
