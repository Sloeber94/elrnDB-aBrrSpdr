Fuse and relay index

Ratings come from the junction-unit tables of the manual (see the table per unit below). After that, every fuse and relay is listed with its rating and every sentence of the manual that names it. The manual writes fuses as F-35 or F35; both are the same fuse. Fuse codes (F1 to F99) are not component codes (F001 to F099, lamps and motors): fuse F35 is not lamp F035.
